GOD BLESSES ISAAC'S FAMILY

Genesis 26
Key Verse: 26:3a

Stay in this land for a while, and I will be with you and will bless you.

* LIFE AMONG HOSTILE PHILISTINES (1-25)

1.  When another famine arose in the land of Canaan, what did Isaac do?  Why
did he plan to go down to Egypt?

2.  What command did God give Isaac? Why? Why might it have  been  hard  for
him to stay in that land?

3.  What promise did God give Isaac? What was the basis of God's promise  to
bless Isaac? What was the condition?

4.  What specific event shows Isaac's fear of the Philistines? What  similar
experience did Abraham have? How was Isaac's family protected from  their
own foolishness? What does this event reveal about Abimelech?

5.  How did the Lord bless Isaac in Gerar? How did  his  wealth  affect  his
relations with the Philistines? How did they harass him?
6.  Why did Isaac finally move to the valley?  How  did  he  deal  with  the
Philistine herdsmen who continually took his wells? How do you  think  he
felt about his life of well-digging?

* DO NOT BE AFRAID; I AM WITH YOU (22-33)

7.  Why was he so insecure and fearful even though no one seemed  to  bother
him in Rehoboth? (22-24)

8.  How did God help him? What is the significance of God's visit to him  at
Beersheba? What is the significance of  Isaac's  building  an  altar  and
calling on the name of the Lord? How does this  event  become  a  turning
point in Isaac's life?

9.  Why did Abimelech come to Isaac? What great change in  Isaac's  relation
with the hostile Philistines does this visit reveal?

10. How did Isaac receive him? What  does  this  event  show  about  Isaac's
quiet victory in the land of hostile enemies? What can we learn from him?
