DAVID PRAISES THE LORD

2 Samuel 21:1-22:51
Key Verses: 22:2-3

1. What problem arose in Israel, what did David do, and what did the LORD
show him? (21:1-2; Jos 9:15,19-20; Nu 30:2) How did David resolve this
matter while keeping his oath to his friend Jonathan? (21:2-9; 1Sa 20:42)
How did he practice compassion while administering justice? (21:10-14a)
How did God bless this? (14b)

2. Note the four enemies who intimidated Israel (15-22). How were they
defeated? How did David's men regard him (17), and what did this mean to
them? (18:3; 23:4)

3. When did David sing this song? (22:1) Read 22:2-3. In what ways did he
describe God, and what do these have in common? (Note how he repeats the
word "my.") How had he learned these things about God through his real-
life experiences? (1Sa 18:11; 20:3; 23: 14,26-29) How have you
experienced God's help personally? Compose a poem of your own.

4. How did David describe his distress, and what did he do to escape it? (4-
7) What can we learn about God from David's vivid description? (8-16) How
did God help him (17-20a), and for what reasons? (20b-25,42; Ps 66:16-20)
What principles of God's way of working can we find here? (26-28) What
was David's conclusion? (29-30)

5. Through God's ways and his words, what confidence did David have in God?
(31-32) How did God arm David (33-37), how did David crush his enemies
(38-43) and how did God exalt him among the nations? (44-46)

6. Read verse 47. In light of all that the LORD had done for him, what did
David exclaim? (cf. Ge 49:24; Dt 32:4,31) Why is God worthy to be
praised? (48-51) After experiencing God's deliverance and victory, what
should we learn about God, and why is it important to praise him?
