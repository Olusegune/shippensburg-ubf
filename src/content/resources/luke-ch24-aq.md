HE HAS RISEN!

Luke 24:1-35
Key Verses: 24:5b-6

1. Read verses 1-8. Who were the women? (10; 8:2-3) Why had they come to
the tomb? What did they find? What glorious sight did they see? How did
they react?

2. What was the message of the angels to them? (When and how do we "look
for the living among the dead"?) What had Jesus prophesied when he was
still in Galilee? How did this confirm his resurrection? What difference
did it make to remember Jesus' words? (8; 9:22,44)

3. Read verses 9-12. What did the women do when they remembered Jesus'
words? How did the apostles respond? What did Peter do?

4. Read verses 13-24. Where were two disciples going? What were they
talking about? Why didn't they recognize Jesus? What facts did they know
about him? What was their broken hope? Why did they need Bible study?

5. Read verses 25-32. How did the Risen Jesus rebuke them? What did he
teach them from the Bible? What Scriptures might he have taught them?
When did they recognize him?

6. Read verses 33-35. Why did these two disciples return to Jerusalem? What
good news did they hear and what good news did they share? Why is it
important for us to believe the resurrection of Jesus?
