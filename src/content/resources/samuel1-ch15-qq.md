TO OBEY IS BETTER THAN SACRIFICE

1 Samuel 15:1-35
Key Verse: 15:22

1. What mission did God give Saul? (1-6; Dt 25:17-19) How did he carry it
out? (7-9)

2. What was in the Lord's heart and why did Samuel cry out? (10-11) In the
meantime, what was Saul doing? (12) How did he rationalize? (13-15)

3. How did Samuel rebuke him? (17-19) What was Saul's problem? (Pr 16:18)
How were his words different from his real motives and actions? (20-21)

4. Read verses 22-23. What was Samuel's divine oracle? As a leader of God's
people, why are obeying and heeding God's word most important? (13:14;
cf. Ac 13:22) What does this mean to you? What are the roots of
disobedience, and what are they equivalent to? (23a)

5. In what respects was Saul's repentance not authentic? (24-31) How did
Samuel obey the Lord absolutely? (32-33) How did Saul's failure affect
both Samuel and the Lord? (34-35)
