Love the Lord Your God

Deuteronomy 5:1-6:25
Key verse: 6:5

I. The Ten Commandments (5:1-33)

1. God made the covenant with the previous generation - how did it relate
to the current generation? (5:1-3) What kind of God is the God who made
the covenant with Israel? (4-6)

2. What is the first commandment and why is it so important? (7)  Why
should we not make an idol in any form? (8-10) Why is it a sin to misuse
God's name? (11)  Why should we observe the Sabbath day and keep it holy?
(12-15)

3. What does God promise to those who honor their parents? (16)  What are
the remaining commandments? (17-21) Think about what it means to truly
love our neighbors.

4. How had Israel responded to God who spoke from the mountain of fire? (22-
27) How would God bless Israel if they kept these commands in the
promised land? (28-33)

II. The Meaning of the Commands and Decrees (6:1-25)

1. For whose benefit did God give his laws, and what were the blessings he
promised? (1-3)

2. Who is the Lord who gives these commands and decrees? (4) How should we
love him? (5) How can we love God with all our heart, with all our soul
and with all our strength?

3. To what extent should we strive to love God and keep his commands? (6-9)

4. In times of blessings, what should we be careful about? (10-19) What
should we teach our children and grandchildren, if they ask about the
meaning of God's commands and decrees? (20-25)
