PAUL'S MINISTRY IN ROME

Acts 28:1-31
Key Verse: 28:31

1. Where did the shipwrecked party land? (1) How were they received by the
islanders? (2) How did God turn a potential tragedy into an opportunity
for ministry? (3-6; Mk 16:18) How did he use Paul there? (7-10)

2. Trace their journey from Malta to Rome. (11-15) How did God encourage
Paul along the way? What was Paul's situation in Rome? (16)

3. Read verses 17-20. How did Paul begin his ministry in Rome? How did he
explain his imprisonment to the Jews? What was the "hope of Israel"?

4. What did the Jews of Rome know about Paul and about the Christian
movement? (21-22) What opportunity did he have to preach to them? (23)
How did he witness to them? What was his message? What do you think he
said about the kingdom of God? About Jesus?

5. What was the response of the Jews? (24) What was Paul's final statement
to them? (25-29) Why did Paul quote Isaiah 6? How did Paul view their
rejection? What can we learn from Paul about how to look at our lives and
ministry?

6. Read verses 30-31. Describe Paul's continuing ministry in Rome. What was
the main content of his preaching? What does this mean? What can we learn
from Paul in Rome about God's way of working?
