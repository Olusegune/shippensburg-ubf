PAUL'S HEART FOR ONESIMUS

Philemon 1-25
Key Verses: 10,11

1. Read verse 1.  What does Paul mean by introducing himself as a prisoner
of Christ Jesus? (See Ac 9:4-6;15; 1Co 15:10a)

2. Read verses 1b-3.  What does this tell us about the recipients of Paul's
letter?  What does he mean by calling Archippus a "fellow soldier"? (Eph
6:12; 2Ti 2:3)

3. Why did the church meet in this home?  What other house church is
mentioned in the New Testament? (Ro 16:3-5; Ac 18:26)  What does this
tell us about the New Testament church?  How can we make our home a house
church?

4. Why are grace and peace mentioned together? (3)  Read verses 4-7.  What
was Paul's thanksgiving topic?  What is faith? (Jn 6:29)  How can one
share it?  What is love? (1Jn 4:16; Ro 8:32)  How can we show it to
others?

5. What was the main point of this letter? (10)  Read verses 8-16.  Who was
Onesimus?  Why was Paul writing about him?

6. What did Onesimus mean to Paul?  What does his name mean?  What had he
done in the past?  How had he been changed?  Why was Paul sending him
back?  Read verse 12 again.  What can we learn here about Paul the
shepherd?

7. Read verse 17-21.  What specific request did Paul make regarding
Onesimus?  How did he back up his request and show how important this was
to him?

8. How did he expect Philemon's relationship with Onesimus to change?  What
does Paul mean by saying, "... knowing that you will do even more than I
ask"? (21)

9. Read verses 22-25.  Where was Paul at this time? (10,13)  What was
Paul's hope and plan?  How does this add weight to his request?

10. What are the people whom Paul introduces?  How does he introduce each
one?  What do you know about each of them?  How does this short letter
reveal the heart of Christ in Paul?
