MAN SHALL NOT LIVE ON BREAD ALONE

Luke 3:21-4:4
Key Verse: 4:4

1. What happened when Jesus was baptized? (21-22) What did God say to Jesus
from heaven? What was the meaning of his baptism? (Mt 3:13-15)

2. Compare and contrast the genealogy of Jesus in Luke (23-38) with that in
Matthew (Mt 1:1-17).

3. How was Jesus led to the desert to be tempted? (1-2) Why did Jesus have
to be tempted by Satan? (Heb 4:15) How did his baptism and temptation
prepare Jesus for his Messianic ministry?

4. What was the first temptation? (3) What was Jesus' physical condition?
(2) Why did the devil begin his temptation with "If you are the Son of
God, ..."? Why was it difficult for Jesus to overcome this temptation?

5. How did Jesus answer the devil? (4) What does it mean "Man shall not
live on bread alone"? (Dt 8:3) Why do we need more than material things
in life? (Mt 6:33; Jn 6:35) What does this mean to you personally?
