THIS IS WHAT THE LORD HAS COMMANDED

Leviticus 9:1-24
Key Verse: 9:6

Then Moses said, "This is what the Lord has commanded you to do, so
that the glory of the Lord may appear to you."

1. When did Moses summon Aaron, his sons and the elders of Israel? (1) What
did Aaron and his sons do for seven days before this? (8:33-36; Ex 29:35-
37) What did Moses tell Aaron to do for himself and the Israelites? (2-6;
Heb 5:1-4) What was the purpose? (4,6)

2. Describe the details of how Aaron made atonement for himself. (7-14) And
for the people. (15-21) Why was it important for him to offer the
sacrifices "in the prescribed way"? (16) What did he do after all was
done? (22)

3. What did Moses and Aaron do together? (23) How did God accept the
offerings? How did the people respond? (24) In what sense was the worship
experience real to them?

4. How many times does the word "commanded" appear in this passage? What
does this teach us about how to worship the Lord?

5. Compare the worship service described in this passage with your worship.
How can we worship God "in the Spirit and in truth"? (Jn 4:24)
