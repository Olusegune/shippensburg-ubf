GOD CALLS ABRAM ABRAHAM

Genesis 16-17
Key verse: 17:5

* ISHMAEL'S BIRTH (16:1-16)

1. Why did Sarai and Abram think it was reasonable, and even God's will,
for Abram to take Hagar as a concubine? What was wrong with this? What
problem arose in Abram's family after Hagar became pregnant? How did
Abram and Sarai deal with this problem?

2. Why did Hagar flee and why did she return? What did she learn about God?
What can we learn about God who helped Abram?

* WALK BEFORE ME AND BE BLAMELESS (17:1-8)

3. How old was Ishmael when God appeared to Abram again? What do you think
Abram's life had been like during those silent years? In what respect had
Abram's faith in the almighty power of God been lacking? Why did God tell
him to "walk before me and be blameless"?

4. How did Abram respond? What did this mean? Why did God change Abram's
name to Abraham? What is the significance of this change? How did God
affirm and expand the covenant promises? What is significant about an
"everlasting covenant?" And "everlasting possession"?

* THE COVENANT SEALED BY CIRCUMCISION (9-27)

5. What did God tell Abraham to do as a sign and seal of the covenant? (9-
14) How did God bless Sarah? What was Abraham's response? Why? What
specific promise did God give Abraham concerning his family? Why did God
choose to give the spiritual blessing to Isaac instead of to Ishmael?

6. How did Abraham demonstrate his belief in God and in God's promises? Why
might this have been difficult for him? What kind of faith does God want
his servants to have? What can we learn in these two chapters about God
and his ways of working?
