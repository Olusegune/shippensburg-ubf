"FOR EVEN THE SON OF MAN"

Mark 10:32-45
Key Verse: 10:45

"For even the Son of Man did not come to be served, but to serve, and
to give his life as a ransom for many."

1. Read verse 32. Describe Jesus and his disciples on their way up to
Jerusalem. Why were the disciples astonished and those who followed
afraid?

2. Read verses 33-34. What did he tell the Twelve? When had he told them
this before? (8:31-32; 9:12; 9:31-32) Why did Jesus repeat this teaching
knowing well that they didn't want to hear it?

3. Read verses 35-36. What did James and John ask Jesus? Why do you think
they chose this time to ask this? What was Jesus' response?

4. Read verses 37-40. What was their request? Why did Jesus say, "You don't
know what you are asking?" What does their "We can" tell us about them?
What did Jesus promise? To what does "cup" and "baptism" refer? What
could he not promise? Why?

5. Read verses 41-45. Why were the ten indignant? What did Jesus teach them
about the difference between disciples and worldly rulers, between
greatness in God's kingdom and greatness in the world?

6. Read verse 45 again. What was Jesus' example? How did Jesus serve? What
can you learn personally from his example?
