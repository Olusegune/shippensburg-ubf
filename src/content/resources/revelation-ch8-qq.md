hope in the midst of calamities

Revelation 8:1-9:21
Key Verse: 9:4

"They were told not to harm the grass of the earth or any plant or tree,
but only those people who did not have the seal of God on their
foreheads."

1. What happens when the Lamb opens the seventh seal? (8:1) What is the
purpose of this extraordinary silence? How do you respond to the truth
that God will not complete his redemptive work until he has heard the
prayers of the saints? (2-4) What does the angel of God do with the
golden censer and why? (5)

2. The trumpets announce that God's saving judgments are implemented at
God's signal. List the events that happen when the first four angels blow
their trumpets. (8:6-12) What similarities do you find between these
events?

3. The eagle proclaims that the last three trumpets are even worse than the
previous four. (8:13) What is more woeful about what follows?

4. What terrible things happen when the fifth angel blows his trumpet? (9:1-
12) Read verse 4. Who are those people who do not have the seal of God on
their foreheads? How does this verse give you hope in the midst of
calamities?

5. What happens when the sixth angel blows his trumpet? (9:13-19) What
shows that the trumpet judgments, like the seal judgments, are not the
final judgment of God but preliminary?

6. What is God's purpose in allowing these judgments to fall on mankind?
(20-21) How can we be saved from the wrath of God?
