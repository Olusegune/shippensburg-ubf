GOD WILL PUNISH IDOLATRY

Micah 1:1-16
Key Verse: 1:2

Hear, O peoples, all of you, listen, O earth and all who are in it,
that the Sovereign LORD may witness against you, the Lord from his
holy temple.

1. Who was Micah? Where was his hometown? What do you know about the reigns
of Jotham, Ahaz and Hezekiah?

2. Read verses 2-5. What can you learn about the sovereign rule of God over
all the earth? Why is his coming fearsome? What was Jacob's
transgression? How did it affect Judah?

3. Read verses 6-7. What was God's warning for Samaria? How did the people
of Samaria prostitute themselves? What would be the wages of their
prostitution?

4. Look at verses 8-16. Why would Micah weep and go about barefoot and
naked? What does it mean the wound reach the very gate of Jerusalem? How
does it mean the transgressions of Israel were found in the Daughter of
Zion? Who would be the conqueror God would bring against his people?

5. What can we learn about God who hates idol worship? How do people commit
the sin of idolatry today?
