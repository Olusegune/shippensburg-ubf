TRUE GREATNESS

Mark 9:30-50
Key Verse: 9:35

Sitting down, Jesus called the Twelve and said, "If anyone wants to be
first, he must be the very last, and the servant of all."

1. Read verses 30-32. Where was Jesus going? What did he teach his
disciples? When had he taught this before? (9:12;8:31) What was the
meaning? (Isa 53:4-6) Why didn't they understand? (Eph 1:18)

2. Read verses 33-34. What had they been arguing about on the road? Why do
men struggle to be the greatest? Read verse 35. What did Jesus teach
about the way to be truly great? How does Jesus himself personify
greatness?

3. Read verses 36-37. What does it mean to welcome a child in Jesus' name?
To welcome Jesus? To welcome the one who sent him?

4. Read verses 38-41. What principle does Jesus teach here? Why is it
important to be generous toward those who do God's work? How can we apply
this?

5. Read verse 42. How serious is it to cause another to sin? Why? Read
verses 43-48. How serious is sin? How can we deal with temptation?

6. Read verses 49-50. What does it mean to be a salty Christian? How can we
be salty? Why must we be salty, and also encourage others to be salty?
