THEY WERE FILLED WITH THE HOLY SPIRIT

Acts 2:1-21
Key Verse: 2:4

1. Read verses 1-4. What do you know about the day of Pentecost? Where were
the believers and what were they doing? What happened? What does this
suggest about the power of the Holy Spirit?

2. Read verses 5-13. Where was the crowd from? What amazed them? What had
happened to those who were filled with the Holy Spirit? What was the
response of the crowd? What is the relationship of this event to the
world mission command of Jesus?

3. Read verses 14-15. What did Peter and the other apostles do? How had
they changed? How did Peter explain why the believers were acting so
strangely?

4. Read verses 16-18. What does it mean for sons and daughters to prophesy;
for young men to see visions and old men to dream dreams?

5. What is the prophesy in verses 19-20? What is the promise in verse 21?
What does it mean to "call on the name of the Lord"?
