SAMUEL'S FAREWELL MESSAGE

1 Samuel 12:1-25
Key Verse: 12:23

1. After confirming Saul as king, what did Samuel say? (1-2) For how long
had he served his people? What do his questions reveal about how he had
lived as their leader? (3-5) (Think about his humility, transparency and
authenticity/integrity.) In his speech, why did he first mention these
things? How did they respond? (5b)

2. How did Samuel remind them of God's deliverance? (6-8) What was their
problem? (9a) How had God disciplined them? (9b) How had they cried out
to the Lord, and how had he answered? (10-11) What can we learn here
about depraved human nature and about God? Why is it important to
remember what God has done personally and nationally?

3. In what sense was it a great sin for them to ask for a king? (12) Even
though they now had a king, what must they and their new king still do?
(13-14) What would happen if they didn't? (15) How did God confirm
Samuel's charge that their asking for a king was evil? (16-18) How did
they respond? (19)

4. How did Samuel comfort and encourage his people, and what direction did
he give them? (20-21) How did Samuel plant hope in them? (22)

5. Read verse 23. Even though he was stepping down as their leader, what
two things did Samuel promise to keep on doing for them, and why? How did
Samuel's commitment reveal God's heart for his people? (2:35) How can we
follow his good example?

6. Read verse 24. For their part, what must the Israelites do? (cf. 14, 20)
How could they possibly serve God faithfully with all their hearts? (24b)
What final warning did Samuel give them and their king? (25)
