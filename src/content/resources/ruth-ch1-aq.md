YOUR GOD WILL BE MY GOD

Ruth 1:1-22
Key Verse: 1:16

But Ruth replied, "Don't urge me to leave you or to turn back from you.
Where you go I will go, and where you stay I will stay. Your people will
be my people and your God my God."

1. Read verses 1-5. What do you know about the time of the judges? (Jdg
17:6; 21:25) What was the relationship between Israel and Moab at that
time? (Jdg 3:28-30) Why do you think Elimelech moved to Moab? (1,6) What
was Naomi's situation as the story begins?

2. Read verses 6-14. Why did Naomi decide to return to Judah? (6) What did
she say to her two daughters-in-law? Think about her selfless attitude in
a desperate situation. What were her prayers for them? Why was her advice
reasonable for them? What did Orpah do?

3. Read verses 15-18. What was Ruth's reply to Naomi? What was she willing
to give up? What was the basis of her decision? What was her faith that
this Gentile woman was included as one of only five women in Jesus'
genealogy? (Mt 1:5)

4. Read verses 19-22. Where did the two women go? In what sense was this
part of God's plan who was working quietly to fulfill his redemptive
purpose? (Mic 5:2) How did Naomi express her bitterness?
