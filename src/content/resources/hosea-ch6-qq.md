our fruitfulness comes from GOD

Hosea 13:1-14:9
Key Verse: 14:8

"O Ephraim, what more have I to do with idols? I will answer him and
care for him. I am like a green pine tree; your fruitfulness comes
from me."

* THERE IS NO SAVIOR BUT GOD (13:1-16)

1. How strong was Israel before she fell into idol worship? (1a) How weak
did the nation become because of its idolatry? (1b-3) What can we learn
about a nation's strength and weakness?

2. How did the Lord save and care for the Israelites? (4-5) Why did they
forget the grace of God? (6) Think about the destructive nature of the
sin of pride. (1Pe 5:5)

3. What would be the consequence of Ephraim's sin? (7-16) What does it mean
God's punishment would be like a lion that attacks and tears apart? What
does God promise in the middle of pronouncing his punishment? (14) How
does God long to redeem and restore sinners? What hope can we have
because of his compassion? (14b; 1Co 15:55)

* TAKE THESE WORDS WITH YOU AND RETURN (14:1-9)

4. What does Hosea encourage sinners to do? (14:1) What are the words we
must take to the Lord? (2-3) How can we offer the fruit of our lips?

5. Read 14:4-8. How does God welcome us when we return to him with
repentance? What does it mean God is like the dew to Israel? Why is it
important to know that our fruitfulness comes from God?

6. What can we learn from Hosea's conclusion? (9) Who can discern the
wisdom? What are the truths you learned from the book of Hosea?
