YOUR CAMP MUST BE HOLY

Deuteronomy 22:9-24:4
Key Verse: 23:14

1. Why shouldn't Israel not mix things when they plant seed, plow the
field, and wear clothes? (9-11; Lev 19:19) Why should they make tassels
on the four corners of their cloak? (12; Nu 15:38-39)

2. How important was sexual purity for the people of Israel? (13-21) How
and why should they deal strictly with sexual sins? (22-30)

3. Who could not enter the assembly of the Lord and for what possible
reasons? (23:1-6) Why could the third generation born to the Edomites and
Egyptians enter the assembly? (7-8)

4. How should Israel keep their camp pure and holy? (9-14) Why should they
not oppress the slaves who take refuge with them? (15-16) Why must they
not bring to the Lord the earnings from prostitution? (17-18)

5. Think about the meanings of the laws concerning charging interest,
fulfilling a vow, and entering neighbor's vineyard and grainfield (19-25)
What was the meaning behind the marriage laws? (24:1-4; cf. Mk 10:1-12)
Why should Israel not bring sin upon the land God was giving them as an
inheritance?
