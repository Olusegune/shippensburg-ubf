"HOW CAN I GIVE YOU UP?"

Hosea 11:1-12:14
Key Verse: 11:8

"How can I give you up, Ephraim? How can I hand you over, Israel? How
can I treat you like Admah? How can I make you like Zeboiim? My heart
is changed within me; all my compassion is aroused."

* GOD'S LOVE FOR ISRAEL (11:1-11)

1. Think about God's love toward his people. (1,3,4) What does it mean God
led them with cords of human kindness and with ties of love? How did they
reject his love? (2)

2. What is Hosea's prophesy about Israel's future? (5-7) What brought their
downfall? Why would a loving God allow the aggressor to invade, conquer
and rule his people?

3. What was God's heart toward his people even as he announced punishment
for them? (8) What do you know about the cities of Admah and Zeboiim? (Dt
29:23) How is our God different from men who come in wrath? (9)

4. How would God teach his people to fear him? (10-11) What does it mean he
would roar like a lion? Think about God who never stops hoping.

* ISRAEL'S DECEIT; GOD'S GRACE (11:12-12:14)

5. How did the chosen people sin against God? (12) What did Ephraim pursue?
(12:1) What was the charge brought against Judah? What does it mean he
will punish Jacob according to his ways? (2-4) How did Jacob struggle
with man and with God? (Ge 32:24-31; Ge 35:15) What does God want us to
learn from this? (5-6)

6. How did Israel boast when she became rich? (7-8) What did God remind
them? (9-14) What are the consequences of ignoring God's word?
