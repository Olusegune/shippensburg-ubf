Fear God and keep his commandments

Ecclesiastes 12:1-14
Key Verse: 12:13

1. How and why do people forget their Creator in the days of their youth?
(1) How did Solomon remember his Creator in his early years as king? (1Ki
3:1-15, 4:29-34; 8:22-61) How did he fail to remember him later? (1K 11:1-
42)

2. What are some of the troubles that people experience as they get old? (2-
5) Why is it important to remember God when everything is meaningless? (6-
8)

3. What did the wise Teacher do for himself and others? (9-10) Given that
Solomon did not follow many of his own advice, how do you evaluate his
wise teachings?

4. In what sense are the words of the wise like sharp objects such as goads
and nails? (11) How are they used by our shepherd? Given so many books
published today, how can we choose them wisely? (12)

5. What is the Teacher's conclusion? (13) What is the duty of mankind? What
is the most important command? (Dt 6:5; Mk 12:29) What is the basis of
God's judgment? (14; Ro 2:6-8; 2Co 5:10) What is your conclusion from the
study of this book?
