MARRIAGE AT THE RESURRECTION

Matthew 22:23-46
Key Verse: 22:30

"At the resurrection people will neither marry nor be given in
marriage; they will be like the angels in heaven."

1. Read verses 23-28. Who were the Sadducees? What question did they ask
Jesus? What did they believe about marriage? About heaven? What kinds of
thoughts occupied their minds?

2. Read verses 29-33. According to Jesus, what was lacking in their faith?
What does this mean? What did Jesus teach about marriage in heaven? What
did Jesus teach from the Bible about Abraham, Isaac and Jacob? About God?

3. Read verses 34-36. How were the Pharisees different from the Sadducees?
Describe their attitude. What question did the Pharisee Bible teacher ask
Jesus? Why did he think that this was a hard question?

4. Read verses 37-40. How did Jesus answer? What is the main point of the
first part of the Ten Commandments? The remaining Commandments? What does
it mean to love God? To love others?

5. Read verses 41-46. How did Jesus silence the religious leaders? What
does Psalm 110 teach about the Christ?
