"BE FAITHFUL TO DEATH"

Revelation 2:1-29
Key Verse: 2:10

Do not be afraid of what you are about to suffer. I tell you, the devil
will put some of you in prison to test you, and you will suffer
persecution for ten days. Be faithful, even to the point of death, and I
will give you the crown of life.

1. Who are the recipients of these letters of Jesus? (See the footnotes for
1,8,12,18). How did the church in Ephesus please the Lord? (1-3)

2. Why is forsaking our "first love" so tragic? (4) Why do you think
Christ's judgment would be so severe if we did not repent? (5)

3. Why do we tend to lose our initial enthusiasm and excitement we had when
we first met Jesus personally? What steps can we take to renew our
commitment to Jesus? What is the promise of Jesus? (7)

4. What did Jesus know about the believers in Smyrna? (9-10) How did they
suffer when they refused to worship the emperor? Why didn't Jesus offer
human sympathy to his suffering people? What does it mean to "be
faithful, even to the point of death"? What are his promises? (10-11)

5. Pergamum was also a center of emperor worship ("where Satan has his
throne"). How did the believers in Pergamum respond to various
temptations? (12-15) What temptations do you face today? What should we
do? (16-17)

6. Consider the letter to Thyatira (18-29). What did the blazing eyes of
Jesus see in the believers of Thyatira? (18-19) Who was Jezebel (1Ki
16:31) and how did the so-called prophetess in Thyatira practice
spiritual adultery similar to Jezebel's? What are Jesus' warning and
promise?
