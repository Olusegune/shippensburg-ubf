PREACH THE WORD

2 Timothy 4:1-22
Key Verse: 4:2

Preach the Word; be prepared in season and out of season; correct,
rebuke and encourage-with great patience and careful instruction.

1. Read verse 1. What shows the solemnity of Paul's charge to Timothy? How
should an awareness of God's presence, of Christ's second coming as
Judge, and of his eternal kingdom affect our attitude toward our mission?

2. Read verse 2. In light of this verse, what is our most important task
while on earth? Why? (See Ro 10:14; 1Co 9:16)

3. How was preaching the word of God the focus of our Lord Jesus' ministry?
Of Paul's? How can we "be prepared"? What does "in season and out of
season" mean? How can we "correct, rebuke and encourage" all at the same
time? Why are "great patience" and "careful instruction" necessary?

4. Read verses 3-4. Why do men "not put up with" and "turn aside" from
sound doctrine? Why are so many people eager to teach and listen to false
doctrine? Read verse 5. What does it mean to "keep your head"? What is
"the work of an evangelist"? How is this related to verse 2?

5. Read verses 6-8. What does it mean to be "poured out like a drink
offering"? What can we learn here about Paul's situation? About his view
of his life? About his faith and hope? What can we learn from him about
how to live the Christian life?

6. Read verses 9-13. Why did these various men leave Paul? Who stayed? Why
might Paul have needed a cloak and parchments? Read verses 14-15. Why did
he warn Timothy about Alexander? Read verses 16-18. What could Timothy
(and we) learn from Paul's faith while on trial?

7. Read verses 19-22. Who were Priscilla and Aquila? (Ac 18:2,3)
Onesiphorus? (2Ti 1: 16-18) Erastus? (Ac 19:22) Trophimus? (Ac 20:4;
21:29) Who were the people around Paul at that time? What does this show
about him? What were his final greetings?
