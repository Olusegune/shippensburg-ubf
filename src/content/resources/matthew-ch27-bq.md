JESUS WAS CRUCIFIED FOR OUR SINS

Matthew 27:32-61
Key Verse: 27:46

About the ninth hour Jesus cried out in a loud voice, "Eloi, Eloi,
lama sabachthani?"--which means, "My God, my God, why have you
forsaken me?"

1. Read verses 32-44. Who was Simon and why did they force him to carry the
cross? Where did the crucifixion take place? What did the soldiers do?
(cf. Ps 22:16-18).

2. What shows Jesus' physical weakness? His resolve? How did the passers-by
taunt him and tempt him? What truth is hidden in the taunts of the
religious leaders? What is the meaning of his suffering to us? (Isa
53:3,4,12; 1Pe 2:24)

3. Why did he refuse to come down from the cross and prove that he is the
Son of God? Why did he refuse to save himself? (Mt 4:5-7; 26:53,54) How
was scripture fulfilled? (Isa 53:12b; Ps 22:7,8)

4. Read verses 45-49. What does it mean that the world became dark? What
did Jesus cry out? What was Jesus' deepest agony and his confidence? (Ps
22:1-3; Isa 59:2; 53:6b,12b)

5. Read verses 50-54. What happened at the moment of Jesus' death? What
does it mean that the curtain of the temple was torn? That tombs broke
open? What was the testimony of the centurion? (Heb 10:19,20)

6. What is the meaning to us and to the world of Jesus' death? (Heb 9:12-
14, 22b,25,26: 1Ti 2:5; Php 2:6-8; Heb 5:8-9)

7. Read verses 55-61. Who witnessed these things? Who was Joseph and how
was he changed? How did he contribute to gospel history?
