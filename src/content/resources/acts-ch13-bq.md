A LIGHT FOR THE GENTILES

Acts 13:14-52
Key Verse: 13:47

1. After leaving Cyprus, where did Paul and Barnabas go? (14) Given the
opportunity to preach, how did Paul begin his message? (16-20) What did
Paul emphasize in the flow of Israel's first 450 years of history? What
was special about King David? (21-25) What was God's promise and how did
he keep it?

2. Read verses 26-31. What are the facts of the message of salvation? What
did God promise David? (32-37) How did God keep that promise? How did God
use David to plant resurrection faith? How did he serve his generation?

3. Read verses 38-39. What is the good news? What is Paul's warning? (40-
41) What was the congregation's initial response? (42-43)

4. What happened the next Sabbath? (44) How did the Jews oppose Paul? (45)
How did Paul and Barnabas answer them? (46-47) What new spiritual
direction did they find from the Scripture? With what result? (48-49)

5. Who stirred up persecution against Paul and Barnabas? (50) How did the
journey team respond? (51) How were the disciples encouraged by their
faith? (52)
