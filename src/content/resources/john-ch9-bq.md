I WAS BLIND BUT NOW I SEE

John 9:13-41
Key Verse: 9:25

1. How had the blind beggar experienced the grace of Jesus? What was his
testimony? Why were the neighbors not so happy to see this joyful event?

2. Read verses 13-17. Why and how did the Pharisees question him? What were
their differing opinions about him? Why? When they asked the former blind
man's opinion, what did he say? What does this mean?

3. Read verses 18-23. Why and how did they question his parents? Why did
they say, "Ask him?" Why were they afraid of being expelled from the
synagogue? What did they lose?

4. When they called him in a second time what did they mean by, "We know
this man is a sinner" and "Give glory to God"? (24) What does this way of
interrogation reveal about the religious leaders?

5. What was the former blind man's answer? Why was it hard and costly for
him to remember God's grace? Think of some examples of others who have
remembered God's grace in costly ways (1Co 15:9). Why is it important to
remember the grace of God?

6. Why did they become so angry? How did they respond? (25-29) What obvious
truth did he teach them? (30-34) How did they punish him? What had this
man lost by sticking to his convictions?

7. How did Jesus help him in his exile? Why is Jesus' way of helping the
best way? What did Jesus reveal about himself and how did the man
respond? What did he gain by remembering Jesus' grace to the end?
