THE MACEDONIAN CALL

Acts 15:36-16:40
Key Verse: 16:9

1. Why did Paul and Barnabas separate? (15:36-41) Where did each go? Who
went with Paul? What does this show about each one? (Col 4:10; 2Ti 4:11)

2. Read 16:1-5. Who was Timothy? (2Ti 1:2-5; Php 2:19-22) Why did Paul
circumcise him? How did Paul's company encourage the churches? What was
the result of their visit?

3. Read verses 6-10. Trace their journey. Where did they try to go? Why did
they not? What happened in Troas? Who joined their journey team? What can
we learn here about God's plans for world mission?

4. Trace their journey to Philippi (11-15). What kind of city was Philippi?
How did they meet their first convert? Who was she and how did she become
a believer? How did she reveal her changed life?

5. Why and how did Paul help a demon-possessed slave girl? (16-24) What
happened as a result? What does this show about the hearts of godless
people?

6. How did Paul and Silas turn a potential tragedy into a great victory?
(25-34) What can we learn from them? Read verses 35-40. How did the
apostles continue to show their courage? How did they encourage the
church in Lydia's house?
