THE SON OF MAN IS COMING

Luke 21:1-38
Key Verse: 21:27

1. Read verses 1-4. Where was Jesus? What did Jesus say about those who
brought gifts to the temple? What spiritual principle did he teach?
Contrast value systems in the spiritual and material worlds.

2. Read verses 5-7. What revealed the disciples' materialism? What did
Jesus teach them about the destiny of the temple (and human
civilization)? What were their two questions? Why?

3. Read verses 8-11. What warning did Jesus give? What are some signs of
the end times? What must we guard against? What are some false messiahs
today?

4. Read verses 12-19. What will happen to those who are faithful to Jesus?
(16-17) Why? What should they do? What will be the result? How can we not
fear? (9; 13-15; 18-19; Lk 9:23)

5. Read verses 20-24. What would happen to Jerusalem? What should God's
people do? From God's point of view, what does this mean?

6. Read verses 25-38. What are some signs of the end? What is the great
sign? Why should we rejoice? Who will not rejoice? What do we learn here
about the word of God? What can we learn from the fig tree? What should
we do?
