DAVID RETURNS TO JERUSALEM

2 Samuel 19:1-20:26
Key Verse: 19:14

1. Look at verses 1-8a. How did David continue to weep and mourn for the
death of his son Absalom? How did God use Joab to help David? How did
David humbly accept his advice?

2. What conflict erupted throughout Israel? (8b-10) How did David win over
the people's hearts? (11-15)

3. How did David deal with Shimei, and why? (16-23) What did he do with
Mephibosheth and Ziba? (24-30) How had Barzillai been a comfort to David?
(31-39)

4. What division had Absalom's rebellion aroused? (19:40-43) How did Sheba
exploit this? (20:1-2) How did David treat his concubines? (20:3) What
assignment did he give Amasa and Abishai? (20:4-7)

5. Why did Joab murder Amasa? (20:8-13) How did one wise woman put an end
to the rebellion? (14-22) Who were David's officials? (23-26)

6. What can you learn from David who overcame his personal animosity and
left avenge to God amid political dramas and intrigues?
