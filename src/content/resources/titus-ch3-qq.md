Devote Yourselves to Doing What is Good

Titus 3:1-15
Key Verse: 3:8

1. Read verses 1-2. What should a spiritual leader remind the people to do?
Why does Paul teach they should be subject to rulers and authorities? (Ac
5:29; Ro 13:1) What does it mean to be ready to do whatever is good? How
can we show true humility toward all men? (Ro 12:3)

2. Read verse 3. How had they (we) once lived? In what sense were they
foolish and disobedient? How were they enslaved by all kinds of passions
and pleasures? Why had they lived in malice and envy? What was the
manifestation of that?

3. Read verses 4-7. What happened to change their behavior? How does God
save us? How is Christ's work of salvation like "washing" and "rebirth"?

4. Read verse 8. What should those who have trusted in God be careful to
do? Why? (14; Eph 2:10) What are the "good" things that we can devote
ourselves to? How can we encourage our co-workers and students to devote
themselves to doing good?

5. Read verses 9-11. What should we avoid? Why? How does Paul tell us to
handle a divisive person? Why?
