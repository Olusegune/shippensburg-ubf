IN GOD'S HANDS

Ecclesiastes 9:1-18
Key Verse: 9:1

1. Contrast the life of the righteous with that of the wicked. (1-2) What
does it mean what the righteous and the wise do are in God's hands? Are
they free from troubles of this world? What is the common destiny that
overtakes all? (2-3)

2. Why is the living better than the dead under the sun? (4-6) What does
the author recommend the living to do despite the common destiny? (7-10)
Why? Compare verse 9 with 1 Corinthians 15:32. Is the author advocating
the Epicurean philosophy that Paul rejected?

3. What other observations does the author make about this world? (11-12)
How true is the statement, "time and chance happen to them all"? (11b)
Given the uncertainty of life, what did David do? (Ps 62:1-2)

4. What is the point of the author's story in verses 13-18? How is wisdom
often despised by people in this world? (Pr 1:7) What is the best wisdom
we can have? (1Co 1:30)
