WHAT IS THE KINGDOM OF GOD LIKE?

Luke 13:18-35
Key Verse: 13:19

1. With what did Jesus compare the kingdom of God? (18-19) What does this
parable teach us about the way the kingdom of God grows?

2. Read verses 20-21. How is the kingdom of God like yeast? What does this
parable teach us about the character of the kingdom of God?

3. Read verses 22-30. Why was Jesus going to Jerusalem? (9:51) What was he
doing along the way? What did someone ask him? How did he answer? What
does "narrow door" imply? In what way is Jesus the narrow door?

4. What does it mean to "make every effort..."? Why is timing important?
What are the consequences of entering or not entering through the narrow
door?

5. Read verses 31-35. What warning did some Pharisees give Jesus? What did
Jesus teach about his mission? Why did Jesus weep over the Holy City?
What was his longing hope?
