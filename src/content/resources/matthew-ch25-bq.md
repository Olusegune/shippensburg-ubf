"WELL DONE, GOOD AND FAITHFUL SERVANT!"

Matthew 25:14-30
Key Verse: 25:21

His master replied, "Well done, good and faithful servant! You have
been faithful with a few things; I will put you in charge of many
things. Come and share your master's happiness!"

1. Read verses 14-18. In this parable, what did the master entrust to each
of his servants? What does it mean that "he went on his journey"? What
was his criteria for deciding how much to entrust to each one?

2. What did each man do with the money? What does it mean that he "put his
money to work?" What was the result of their efforts?

3. Read verses 19-23. When the master returned to settle accounts, what did
the first two servants report? What was the Master's response to each?
Why did he call them "good and faithful"? What reward was given to each
one?

4. Read verses 24-25. What did the third man know about his master? What
led him to bury his talent in the ground?

5. Read verses 26-30. How did the master respond to his report? Why did the
master not accept the third man's report and excuses? What did the master
command to be done? Why was he so severe with this man?

6. What was Jesus teaching through this parable? What can we learn here
about how to live as we wait for Jesus' return?
