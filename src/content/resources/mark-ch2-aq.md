JESUS' POWER TO FORGIVE SINS

Mark 2:1-22
Key Verse: 2:10

"But that you may know that the Son of Man has authority on earth to
forgive sins...."

1. Read verses 1-2. Where had Jesus been, and what had he been doing just
before? How did people respond when they heard that he had returned to
Capernaum? How did he minister to them?

2. Read verses 3-5. Why could the men carrying a paralytic not get to
Jesus? What did they do? What did Jesus think about their action? What
does this teach us about faith?

3. What do Jesus' words to the paralytic suggest about his real problem?
About Jesus' love? How is paralysis like sin? In what respect might he be
called a sinner?

4. Read verses 6-12. What did the teachers of the law think about Jesus'
words? Why? What did Jesus say? What did he do? What did he teach about
himself? How did the people respond?

5. Read verses 13-17. What does Levi's business tell us about him? What was
Jesus' invitation? How did Levi respond? Why? How did Levi celebrate his
new life? Why did the Pharisees criticize Jesus? How did Jesus answer?
What does this mean?

6. Read verses 18-19. Why did some people criticize Jesus' disciples? How
did Jesus answer? What does it mean that Jesus is the bridegroom? When
should we fast?

7. Read verses 20-22. How were the disciple of Jesus like new wineskins and
new patch? How were Pharisees like old wineskins? What does Jesus teach
us here? How can we be new wineskins?
