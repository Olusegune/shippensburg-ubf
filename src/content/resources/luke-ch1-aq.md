THE FAITH OF ZECHARIAH AND ELIZABETH

Luke 1:1-25
Key Verse: 1:6

1. What was Luke's motivation in compiling the gospel account? (1-4) Why
were the birth, life and death of Jesus such epochal events in human and
biblical history? (1-4)

1. In what respects was Zechariah's life of faith like a lamp burning in
dark times? What shows the darkness of the times? What does it mean they
were "righteous in the sigh of God"? (5-6)

2. Describe Zechariah's encounter with the angel Gabriel. What does this
event reveal about Zechariah's prayer life? (8-13)

3. Why was John called great before God? (14-17)

4. Why was Zechariah rebuked by the angel Gabriel? How did God train him
and what was the purpose of the training? What was Elizabeth's confession
of faith? What does this show about her faith?
