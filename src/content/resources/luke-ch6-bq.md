JESUS CHOOSES THE TWELVE APOSTLES

Luke 6:12-26
Key Verse: 6:13

1. What was the growing threat to Jesus and his "new wineskin" ministry?
(11) Read verse 12. What did Jesus do? What might have been his prayer
topic? (Lk 11:2) What do you learn about prayer from Jesus?

2. Read verse 13. On what basis did Jesus choose his apostles? Why might
Jesus have chosen twelve of them? What does the designation "apostle"
tell about Jesus' purpose in choosing them?

3. Read verses 14-16. Who was Simon? (Lk 4:38-39; 5:1-11) Why did Jesus
change Simon's name to Peter? What does this suggest about the importance
of one person? (Mt 16:18) Think about Andrew (Jn 6:8), James, John (Mt
20:20-21), and the others. Why might Jesus choose such a diverse group?

4. Read verses 17-19. Describe the scene of Jesus' presentation of the
twelve apostles. Read verses 20-23. According to Jesus' words, who are
the blessed? Why are they blessed? What basic attitude must disciples of
Jesus have?

5. Read verses 24-26. According to Jesus' words, for whom are woes
reserved? Why? Why is it important for his disciples to accept his
teaching about woes?
