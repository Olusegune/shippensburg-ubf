PAUL'S HOPE IN THE RESURRECTION

Acts 24:1-27
Key Verse: 24:15

1. How did Paul come to be in Caesarea? (1-4) Why did Ananias and Tertullus
and other religious leaders go there? Who was Felix?

2. What charges did Tertullus bring against Paul? (5-9) How did Paul begin
his answer to the charges? (10-13)

3. Read verses 14-16. What did he admit? What did he say that was similar
to his statements to the Sanhedrin? (23:1,6) What did he mean by saying
that he is a "follower of the Way"? What was his hope? How did it affect
his life?

4. How did Paul describe what happened in Jerusalem? (17-21) What did Felix
decide to do after hearing Paul's factual testimony? (22-23) Why was he
so indecisive?

5. Why do you think Felix wanted to listen to Paul? (24) How did Paul use
this opportunity to talk to Felix? (25) What was the content of his
preaching? What can we learn from Paul? How did Felix respond? What can
we know about his character? (26-27)
