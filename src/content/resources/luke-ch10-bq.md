WHO IS MY NEIGHBOR?

Luke 10:25-37
Key Verse: 10:28

1. Read verses 25-26. What did the expert in the law ask Jesus? Why? What
problem did he have in his heart? With what question did Jesus counter?
How did Jesus invite the man to share his personal understanding of the
Bible?

2. Read verses 27-28. What was the man's answer? Why was it correct? (Dt
6:5; Lev 19:18) How did Jesus respond? What did the man need to do? How
would this solve his inner problem?

3. Read verse 29. What was the man's second question and why did he ask it?
What does this show about him? What answer do you think he expected?

4. Read verses 30-35. How did Jesus answer the man? In the parable, who
were the first two people to see the wounded man and what did they do?
How might they have justified themselves? (Nu 19:11) What does this
suggest about the spiritual condition of religious leaders of that time?

5. How did the Samaritan help the injured man? What did he lose in time,
money and effort to help this man? What were the dangers involved? What
does this show about the Samaritan? Why do you think Jesus chose a
Samaritan to be the hero of this story?

6. Read verses 36-37. What was Jesus' point in telling this parable? How is
this related to verse 28? How is Jesus like the Good Samaritan? Who is
your neighbor and how are you taking care of him/her?
