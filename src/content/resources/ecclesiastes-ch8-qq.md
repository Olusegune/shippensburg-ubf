ENJOY LIFE AS YOU SERVE GOD

Ecclesiastes 8:1-17
Key Verse: 8:15

1. How does wisdom change our outlook of the world? (1) Why should we obey
the authorities of this world? (2-6; Ro 13:1,7)

2. Read verses 7-8. How is death an equalizer for the powerful and the
wicked alike? What does it mean "wickedness will not release those who
practice it"? (7b) Given the uncertainty of future and the certainty of
death, how should a wise person live?

3. What will happen eventually to those who are wicked and powerful? (9-10)
Why is it better to fear the Lord while the wicked take advantage of the
imperfect criminal justice system? (11-13)

4. How unfair is life on earth? (14) What does the author recommend to
God's people? (15; Ps 42:8; Php 4:12) Why? (16-17) How can we apply this
to our daily life?
