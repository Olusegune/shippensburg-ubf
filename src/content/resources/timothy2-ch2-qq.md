A WORKMAN APPROVED BY GOD

2 Timothy 2:1-26
Key Verse: 2:15

Do your best to present yourself to God as one approved, a workman who
does not need to be ashamed and who correctly handles the word of
truth.

1.    Read verse 1. What was the relationship between Paul and Timothy?
(See also 1:1-2) What is the grace that is in Christ Jesus? (See 1:8-9;
Eph 2:5,8; 1Co 15:10) What does it mean to be strong in this grace?

2.    Read verses 1-2. What does Paul teach Timothy by word and example
about raising disciples? How can one be qualified to teach others?

3.    Read verses 3-7. What should we learn from the soldier, the athlete,
the farmer? How can we please our commanding officer? What are civilian
pursuits? How can we receive the victor's crown? How can we qualify to
receive a share of the farmer's crops?

4.    Read verses 8-13. What must we always remember? How important was it
to Paul to not compromise the gospel? What does it mean that "God's word
is not chained"? What does it mean to "endure?" (3,10,12) How can we
endure? What is our hope? (11-13)

5.    Read verses 14-19. Of what must we be reminded and warned? What's
wrong with quarreling about words? Why is godless chatter bad? (14,16)
What was dangerous about the teaching of Hymenaeus and Philetus?

6.    Read verse 15 again. What is the Christian's best defense against
godless and false teaching? What does it mean to correctly handle the
word of truth? How can we? How can we be workmen approved by God?

7.    Read verses 20-21. How is this large house a parable of a church?
What can we learn from these verses? How can we be instruments ready for
the Master's use? (22-26)

8.    Read verses 22-26 again. What else can you learn here about the life
of the Lord's servant? About his attitude toward coworkers? About his way
of working and his prayer topics?
