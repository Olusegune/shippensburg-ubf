THE KINGDOM OF GOD HAS COME UPON YOU

Luke 11:14-28
Key Verse: 11:20

1.    Where was Jesus going and why? (9:51) What did he take time to do?
(14) Why? What are some of the challenges in life facing a man who is
mute? How was his life changed by Jesus? How did the crowd respond?

2.    Read verses 15-16. What did some doubters say? Who were they? (Mt
12:24; Mk 3:22) Read verses 17-20. What did Jesus teach them? What did
Jesus teach about the finger of God? About Jesus' source of power?

3.    Read verses 21-23. Who is the strong man? Who is the stronger man?
How did Jesus try to help even the crooked religious leaders to believe
so that they could enter the kingdom of God?

4.    Read verses 24-26. What danger do those face who try to clean up
their lives without Jesus and without the word of God? Why must a
repentant and forgiven sinner hold Jesus' words in his heart?

5.    Read verses 27-28. What did Jesus teach the admiring woman about what
is really important?
