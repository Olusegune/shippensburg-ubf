SAMUEL ANOINTS DAVID

1 Samuel 16:1-23
Key Verse: 16:7

1. What was Samuel doing for Saul? (1a) How did the Lord direct Samuel in
choosing a new king? (1-3) What was the potential danger of anointing
another king while Saul remained as the king of Israel?

2. How did Samuel obey? (4) How did the elders of Bethlehem react to
Samuel's sudden visit? (5) How did Samuel reply to them? (5)

3. Who impressed Samuel at first? (6) What was Samuel's initial conclusion?
How did God warn Samuel against judging by appearance alone? (7) Knowing
that God judges a person by faith and character, not appearance, what
steps should we take to build our inner character?

4. How many sons did Jesse have them pass in front of Samuel? (8-10) What
did Samuel say to each of them? Which son was not there? (11) Why?

5. How is David described? (12a, 18) What did God say to Samuel? (12b) Who
chose David as the next king of Israel? (1b, 12b) What happened when
David was anointed? (13, Ac 1:8)

6. What were the consequences of Saul's disobedience to God? (14) How did
he attempt to alleviate his tormented soul? (15-17)

7. How did David become Saul's attendant? (17-23) What job did Saul give to
David? (21) How did God train David under Saul to become the next king?
