SALVATION IS FOUND IN NO ONE ELSE

Acts 4:1-31
Key Verse: 4:12

1. Read verses 1-4. Why were the Jewish authorities so disturbed by the
apostles' preaching? How did they try to stop them? What was the result
of the apostles' preaching?

2. Read verses 5-7. Before whom were Peter and John brought? What do you
know about these Jewish leaders? (Jn 18:13,14) What did they ask Peter
and John?

3. Read verses 8-11. What did Peter say about the man who had been healed?
What did he say about Jesus? What did he say about those leaders' role in
Jesus' crucifixion?

4. Read verse 12. What is the climax of his testimony? How can he say that
there is no other way of salvation other than Jesus? (11-12; Jn 14:6) Can
we who live in a multi-cultural society say this? Why or why not?

5. Read verses 13-22. What did the rulers realize about Peter and John?
What was the source of the apostles' strength, courage and wisdom? (13)
What was the dilemma of the religious leaders? (14-17) What did they do?
What was the apostles' response? Why did the religious leaders let them
go? (18-22)

6. Read verses 23-31. From what perspective did the believers see these
events? How was Scripture fulfilled? What was their prayer topic? God's
response? What can we learn from them?
