USE WEALTH TO GAIN FRIENDS FOR ETERNITY

Luke 16:1-18
Key Verse: 16:9

1. Read verses 1-7. To whom did Jesus tell this parable? (1,14) Why did the
rich man decide to fire his manager? How did the manager evaluate his
situation? What did he decide to do?

2. Read verses 8-9. Why did his master (a shady character himself) commend
the dishonest manager? What is the point of Jesus' teaching? (Mt 10:16)
What do the words "worldly wealth" and "eternal dwellings" mean? For what
should we (the people of the light) use worldly wealth? What does this
mean to you?

3. Read verses 10-12. What do these verses teach about the essential
quality of a good and wise manager? (1Co 4:2) How can you be a
trustworthy manager of your God-given resources?

4. Read verses 13-15. Why can't we serve both God and money? (Mt 6:24) What
must be the value system of God's people? What is the spiritual problem
of the Pharisees? How did they justify themselves? What spiritual
principle does Jesus teach us?

5. Read verses 16-18. What is the difference between the times before and
after John? What does it mean that everyone is forcing his way into the
kingdom? (RSV: "everyone enters it violently.") What is Jesus' view of
the Bible? What epitomizes unfaithfulness?
