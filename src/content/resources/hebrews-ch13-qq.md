"KEEP ON LOVING"

Hebrews 13:1-25
Key Verse: 13:1

* KEEP ON LOVING EACH OTHER (1-6)

1.    What three things must Christians keep on doing? (1-3)

2.    What does it mean to love each other as brothers?  What does Jesus
say about this? (Jn 13:34,35; Mt 5:46) Why does he say "keep on"? (Mt
24:12) What is Jesus' example? (8; Jn 13:lb)

3.    Who entertained strangers and found that he was entertaining angels?
What can we learn from him? (Ge 18:1-8) Why is it important for
Christians to show hospitality?

4.    What should be our attitude toward those in prison?  What does this
mean?  What does it mean to suffer with those who are mistreated?  Why
should we do this?  How can we do this?

5.    Read verses 4-6.  What tempts worldly people to be discontent?  What
does the Bible say about sexual immorality?  About marriage?  Why is
sexual immorality such a serious sin?

6.    What should be our attitude toward money?  Why should God's people
not love money? (1 Ti 6:10; Mt 6:24) How can we learn to be content with
what we have? (5,6)

* CONFESS HIS NAME (7-25)

7.    What does it mean to "Remember your leaders"?  What is the mark of a
leader?  How can we imitate their faith? (Heb 11:1-8) Who is the best one
to imitate? (8) How is Jesus' faithfulness described?

8.    Read 9-14.  What strengthens our faith and what does not?  How is
Jesus different from priests who served in the temple?  How and why did
Jesus suffer?  How can we share in his sufferings? (13; 1 Pe 4:13)

9.    What are the sacrifices that please God?  How can we offer such
sacrifices? (15,16; 1 Th.5:16-18; Ac.16:24,25) What does it mean to
"confess his name"? (Mt 26:35,72; 1 Pe 2:9; 1Ti 1:15b)

10.   Why should we obey our leaders?  How should we pray for them?  What
is the author's prayer for them? (17-25) What can we learn here about how
God works in and through his people?
