GOD TRAINS HIS PEOPLE

Judges 1:1-3:6
Key Verse: 3:4

1. As the book of  Judges  opens,  what  was  the  political  and  spiritual
situation of the Israelites? How did they prostitute themselves to  other
gods? Why? How did God help them? (1:1; 2:1-23; 3:1-6; 21:25)

2. Why  was  it  necessary  for  them  to  continue  to  fight  against  the
Canaanites? Review the victories that the Israelites won in their battles
against the Canaanites: the southern  territory  (Bezek,  Jerusalem,  the
Negev, Hebron, Debir,  Zephath,  Gaza,  Ashkelon,  Ekron);  the  northern
territory (Bethel).

3. Review also their failures. What had been  God's  clear  instructions  to
them in dealing with their enemies? (Ex  23:31-33;  Dt  7:1-5;  20:16-18)
What were the factors involved in their  failures?  (1:19;  22-36;  2:11-
14,19; 3:5-6)

4. What are the enemies we are called to  drive  out  today?  What  is  your
enemy that you are yet to drive out completely from your life? Why  can't
God and the enemy live together in our lives?

5. What was the vicious cycle of sin or pattern of national  decline  during
the period of the Judges? (2:10-19) How did God  train  them?  (2:20-3:4)
What can you  learn  from  this  passage  about  seeking  an  easy-going,
comfortable lifestyle versus fighting spiritual battles in  obedience  to
God's word?
