I HAVE BEEN CRUCIFIED WITH CHRIST

Galatians 2:11-21
Key Verse: 2:20

I have been crucified with Christ and I no longer live, but Christ
lives in me. The life I live in the body, I live by faith in the Son
of God, who loved me and gave himself for me.

1. How did the circumcision group influence Peter to change his behavior,
and what impact did this have on the Jewish Christian community? (12-13)
How and why did Paul rebuke him publicly? (14) In this case, what does it
mean to "act in line with the truth of the gospel"? Why was it so
important for Jewish Christians to have fellowship with Gentile
Christians? (3:26-29)

2. Read verses 15-16. How does the gospel apply to both Jews and Gentiles,
and what contrast does Paul make here? How do people generally understand
the term "justified," and how do they pursue it? What does it mean to be
justified only by faith in Christ? (Ac 13:38-39; Ro 1:17; 3:24-25)

3. How did Paul dispel misunderstandings about those who trust only in the
gospel? (17-18) Read verse 19. What did he mean when Paul said he died to
the law? (Ro 7:4) For what purpose? (19b)

4. Read verse 20. What does it mean to be "crucified with Christ"? (Ro 6:3-
4) How was Paul changed from a self-centered to a Christ-centered person?
(1Ti 1:13-14; 2Co 5:17; Ro 14:7-8) How did Christ's love enable him to
live by faith? (20b) In light of this, why is it so important to know
Jesus personally, not just theoretically?

5. Read verse 21. What two ways of living after receiving Christ does Paul
contrast here? What consequence does Paul mention? Why is it important to
continue to live by the grace of God?

6. How can we live only by faith in the grace of Jesus in our personal
lives? How can we apply this to the way we relate to others in our field
of ministry?
