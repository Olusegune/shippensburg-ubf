RUTH'S OBEDIENCE

Ruth 3:1-18
Key Verse: 3:5

"I will do whatever you say," Ruth answered.

1. Read verses 1-4. What did Naomi tell Ruth to do? Why? What does it mean
"to find a home"? (How do you think Naomi knew Boaz would be spending the
night at the threshing floor?) What can we learn from Naomi who was
looking out for the best interests of her daughter-in-law? Why did this
task require courage and faith from Ruth?

2. Read verses 5-9. What was Ruth's answer to Naomi? (5) What can we learn
from her obedience? (6) How did she carefully follow Naomi's
instructions? What did it mean to spread the corner of your garment over
someone? (9) How do you know this wasn't a romantic gesture?

3. Read verses 10-13. What was Boaz's reaction when he found out it was
Ruth lying at his feet? What were his first words to her? How did he
speak of her great kindness and noble character? (10-11) What showed that
Boaz had already investigated the possibilities of redeeming Ruth? (12-
13) How was he ready to act?

4. Read verses 14-15. In what sense was Boaz a man of integrity? How did he
show his kindness and generosity toward Ruth once again?

5. Read verses 16-18. What was Naomi's reaction when Ruth reported what had
happened at the threshing floor? What can we learn about God who was
opening a way for the two women of faith?
