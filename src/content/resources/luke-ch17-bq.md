JESUS BLESSES A THANKFUL SAMARITAN

Luke 17:11-19
Key Verses: 17:17-19

1. Read verses 11-12. Where was Jesus going? Whom did he meet at the border
between Samaria and Galilee? What was the common bond between this group
of Jews and Samaritans?

2. Read verses 13-14. How did they show respect and trust toward Jesus?
What do you think the lepers expected when they called out to Jesus for
mercy? What did Jesus tell them to do? What happened when they obeyed?

3. Why should they show themselves to the priests? (Mk 1:44; Lev 13:1-46)
What revealed their faith? (Luke makes the point that the lepers weren't
healed until they started to obey Jesus' command)

4. Read verses 15-16. In what ways was one man different from the rest? How
did his actions show about his thankful heart? To whom did he attribute
his healing?

5. Read verses 17-19. When Jesus said to him "Rise and go; your faith has
made you well," was he speaking of the leper's physical healing, his
spiritual salvation, or both? Were the other nine saved or healed
spiritually? Why or why not? What lesson did Jesus teach?

6. What does this event teach about the importance of being thankful? Why
is it so important? Can you think of any other examples of thankful
people in the Bible? Why is it easy for us to forget to thank God for his
blessings? What is the result of being thankful?
