SO THAT INNOCENT BLOOD WILL NOT BE SHED

Deuteronomy 19:1-22:8
Key Verse: 19:10

1. The Israelites were told to set aside three cities in the Promised Land.
How and for what purpose? (1-3) What was the rule concerning those who
kill a person and flee to a city of refuge? (4-13) What does this teach
us about God's view of human life?

2. Why should the Israelites not move their neighbor's boundary stone? (14)
How many witnesses were required to convict a person for any crime? (15)
What should the judges do if a malicious witness gave false testimony
against a fellow Israelite? (16-21)

3. Why shouldn't God's people be afraid of their enemies when they go to
war? (20:1) What message should the priest give to the army before going
into battle? (2-4) Who should be sent home? (5-9)

4. What were the guidelines for attacking their enemies that were at a
distance from them? (10-15) How about the nations that were in the
Promised Land? (16-18) Why should they not cut down fruit-bearing trees?
(19-20)

5. If someone is found slain by an unknown murderer, what should the elders
and judges do to mourn and pray for atonement for the entire community?
(21:1-9) What does breaking the neck of a heifer look forward to? What
were the guidelines for marrying a captive woman? (10-14) What was the
right of the firstborn? (15-17) What should be done to a rebellious son?
(18-21)

6. Why should not a murderer's body be left hanging on a pole overnight?
(22-23) If they saw their fellow Israelite's animals straying or injured,
what should they do? (21:1-4) Consider the various laws in verses 5-8.
Which ones were common sense? Which ones were to promote safety?
