THE COST OF DISCIPLESHIP

Luke 14:25-35
Key Verses: 14:26-27

1. Read verses 25-27. To whom did Jesus give these words? How are crowds
different from disciples? What must one overcome in order to be Jesus'
disciple?

2. What does he mean by "hate"? "Carry their cross"? (9:22-23) How did
Jesus carry his cross? In what way is the invitation to disciples like
the invitation to the great banquet? (15-24)

3. Read verses 28-33. What one point do these two illustrations teach? What
is the cost of following Jesus? How is discipleship a life-time
commitment? Why is it worth to give up everything for? (33) What does
this mean to you personally?

4. Read verses 34-35. What are major functions of salt? What does it mean
to lose our saltiness? How can we keep our Christian flavor and preserve
the gospel in our generation?
