KNOW THAT GOD IS THE JUDGE

Ecclesiastes 11:1-10
Key Verse: 11:9

1. What is the meaning of verses 1-2? (Compare different translations) How
can we act wisely when we do not know what the future holds? (Pr 19:17;
Mt 10:42)

2. What is the author's warning to those who make excuses for not doing
their proper work? (3-4)

3. Read verses 5-8. Notice how many times the phrase "you do not know
(understand)" is repeated. Given our short lifetime, what should we do
rather than try to figure out everything? What does "Sow your seed in the
morning" mean to you personally? (Gal 6:8-10)

4. Read verses 9-10. How can young people enjoy their youth and still
remember that they must give an account to God for everything they do?
What does it mean that youth and vigor are meaningless? (The Hebrew term
hebel can refer to a "vapor" or "mere breath")
