YOU ARE A PEOPLE HOLY TO THE LORD

Deuteronomy 14:1-16:17
Key Verse: 14:2

1. As the children of God, the Israelites were told not to cut themselves
or shave the front of their heads as they mourned the dead (14:1). Why
not? What was their identity? (2) Why shouldn't they follow the
lifestyles of the pagans?

2. What were the animals that the Israelites were allowed to eat? (3-21)
Which ones were not allowed? What was the spiritual meaning behind these
dietary restrictions? (21a) How can we apply this to our lives today?

3. What should God's people dedicate to the Lord? (22; Lev 27:30) How were
they to eat the tithes together as a community and support the Levites
and the less fortunate with the tithes? (23-29)

4. What should Israel do with debts every seven years? (15:1-3) What was
God's promise for them? (4-6) How should they help the poor and the needy
among them? (7-11)

5. How should Israel set their servants free every seven years? (12-14)
Why? (15) What if a servant didn't want to leave the family? (16-18) What
were the Israelites to do with the firstborn animals? (19-23)

6. How should Israel celebrate the Passover? (16:1-8) What should they
remember as they celebrated the Festival of Weeks? (9-12) How were they
to celebrate the Festival of Tabernacles? (13-15) How should they come to
the Lord during these three festivals? (16-17)
