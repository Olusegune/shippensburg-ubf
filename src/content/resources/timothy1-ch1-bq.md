CHRIST JESUS CAME INTO THE WORLD

1 Timothy 1:12-20
Key Verse: 1:15

1. Read verses 12-14. For what was Paul thankful to God? (12) What kind of
man was he? How did God show his mercy? (13) What were the gifts of grace
by which God changed Paul? (14) What can we learn here about God's grace?

2. Read verses 15-16. What did Paul know about himself? Why is it necessary
for every believer to know what kind of sinner he was? What did Paul
learn about Jesus? When Paul thought about Jesus what doxology of praise
welled up in his heart? (17)

3. Read verses 18-19. Why did Paul tell Timothy about all of his past sins?
How did he view the Christian life? In what sense is the life of faith a
battle? Who is our enemy? (Eph 6:12)

4. What does it mean to 'fight the good fight of faith'? How can we? What
happened to those who gave up the good fight? (19b-20)

5. When God's enemies rule the world how can Timothy-like soldiers fight
the good fight of faith? What was Paul's secret? (15)
