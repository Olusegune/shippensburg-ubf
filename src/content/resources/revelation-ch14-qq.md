THE TIME OF HARVEST

Revelation 14:1-20
Key Verse: 14:6-7

Then I saw another angel flying in midair, and he had the eternal gospel
to proclaim to those who live on the earth--to every nation, tribe,
language and people. He said in a loud voice, "Fear God and give him
glory, because the hour of his judgment has come. Worship him who made
the heavens, the earth, the sea and the springs of water."

1. What does John see standing on Mount Zion? (1) Who is with him, and who
do they represent? (5:12; 7:14) What does John hear? (2)

2. In John's vision, what were the 144,000 singing, and where? (3) Who
could learn the song? How are the 144,000 described? How can we keep
ourselves pure? (4-5)

3. What did the first of three angels have? What did it say? (6-7) What did
the second angel say? (8) What did the third angel say? (9-11) What do
these proclamations reveal about God's desire to save all sinners of the
world?

4. What does "the patient endurance on the part of the saints" mean? (12)
What did a voice from heaven say? What did the Spirit say? (13) How does
this promise encourage us to remain faithful?

5. In the first of two harvests, who did the harvesting and what was
harvested? (14-16) How is this harvest a time of joy for the believers
who remain faithful to Jesus? (Jn 5:24; Mt 13:37-43)

6. In the second harvest, who did the harvesting and what was reaped? (17-
18) What was done with the grapes? What was produced? (19-20) What does
this symbolize? (Joel 3:12-13 ) How can we be ready for this time of
harvest?
