THE LORD CALLS SAMUEL

1 Samuel 2:12-4:1a
Key Verse: 3:10

1. What was the root cause of Eli's son's wickedness? (2:12b) How did they
abuse their priestly office? (13-17; cf. Lev 7:31) Why was their sin so
great in God's sight? (2:17)

2. On the other hand, what was Samuel doing? (2:18) How did Hannah support
him? (2:19) How did God bless that family? (2:20-21)

3. What did Eli hear about his sons and how did he deal with this? (2:22-
25a) How did his sons respond to him and why? (2:25b) In this
environment, how did God bless the boy Samuel? (2:26)

4. Of what privileges did a man of God remind Eli? (2:27-28) For what did
he rebuke him? (2:29) What judgment did God pronounce upon his family and
his sons? (2:30-34, 36) What kind of priest would God raise in their
place? (2:35) Read 2:30b. What can we learn here about God in his dealing
with this problem?

5. How are those days described? (3:1) What was the main problem? In 3:2-3
how is the setting described? How did the Lord intervene? (3:4a) How did
Samuel respond? (3:4b-6) Why? (3:7) When did Eli figure out what was
going on, and how did he instruct Samuel? (3:8-9)

6. Read 3:10. What can we learn about God who persistently called Samuel by
name? Describe Samuel's response to God's calling (3:10b). How does this
reveal his attitude of readiness? Think about the importance of listening
to God.

7. What message did the Lord give Samuel? (3:11-14) Why would this be
difficult for him to hear and even more difficult to tell? (3:15) How
could he say everything to Eli, hiding nothing? (3:16-18) What spiritual
lesson did Samuel learn through this experience? How did God establish
him as a prophet of his word? (3:19-4:1a)
