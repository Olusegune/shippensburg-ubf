SAUL, THE FIRST KING OF ISRAEL

1 Samuel 10:1-11:15
Key Verse: 10:24

1. What did Samuel do to make Saul king? (10:1) What signs would confirm
Saul's anointing? (10:2-6)

2. What instructions did Samuel give? (10:7-8) How were these signs
fulfilled? (10:9-16) What is the significance of Saul's being changed and
prophesying?

3. When he gathered all Israel at Mizpah, how did Samuel remind them of
God's grace and rebuke them? (10:17-19)

4. How did God himself demonstrate that he had chosen Saul? (10:20-24) How
did the people respond? (10:24b) What can we learn in these events about
Samuel's obedience to God? (8:6; 9:22-10:1)

5. What did Samuel teach from God's law about the kingship? (10:25; Dt
17:14-20) What happened when Saul went home? (10:26-27)

6. Who besieged Jabesh Gilead? (1) How did the Ammonite insult the men of
Jabesh? (2)   How did the elders of Jabesh react? (3-4)

7. How did Saul react to this bad news? (5)What was his source of power?
(6) How did Saul turn out the Israelites as one man? (7) What was his
leadership style?

8. How did Saul win his first victory? (9-11) In what sense did this
victory vindicate the choice of Saul as king? (12) How did Saul interpret
the victory? (13b) How did Samuel confirm Saul's kingship? (14-15)
