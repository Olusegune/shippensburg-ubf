"GIVE TO GOD WHAT IS GOD'S"

Mark 12:13-17
Key Verse: 12:17

"Then Jesus said to them, 'Give to Caesar what is Caesar's and to God
what is God's.' And they were amazed at him."

1. Read verses 13-14a. Who did the religious leaders send to Jesus? For
what purpose? (12; 11:27) How did they flatter Jesus? Why? Was what they
said about him true? If so, how could they be his enemies?

2. Who were the Pharisees? (See Mt 23:24; Lk 11:46; Mt 23:5; Mk 12:40) How
could they become so corrupt? Who were the Herodians? Why was it unusual
for the Pharisees and the Herodians to be doing something together?

3. Read 12:14b-15. What was the loaded question they asked him? (14b-15)
What was the motive behind their question? Read verses 15b. What did
Jesus know about them? Why did he ask for a coin?

4. Read 12:16-17. Whose portrait and whose inscription were on the coin?
What did Jesus mean by "Give to Caesar what is Caesar's"? What belongs to
Caesar? How can we apply this to our practical lives?

5. What belongs to God that we should give him? (Dt 6:5) What has God given
to us? How can we be people who give to others rather than people who try
to get from others? (See Jn 3:16; Mk 10:45; Ac 20:35)
