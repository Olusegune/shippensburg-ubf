THERE IS NO ONE RIGHTEOUS, NOT EVEN ONE

Romans 3:1-20
Key Verse: 3:10

1. Read verses 1-4. What is the advantage of being a Jew, or of being born
into a Christian home? Why is being entrusted with God's laws (words of
God) such a blessing? Does one's unfaithfulness nullify God's
faithfulness?

2. Read verses 5-8. If some people thought that since their unrighteousness
brought out God's righteousness more clearly, what argument could they
make? What is wrong with people who say things like, "The loving God
wouldn't condemn me," or "I am not a bad person"? Why is their
condemnation just?

3. Read verses 9-11. What does it mean to be righteous before God? Why is
there no one righteous, not even one? Do you consider yourself a sinner
before God? What sins have you committed?

4. Read verses 12-18. What do these Scriptures from the Old Testament teach
us about humanity under the power of sin? Think about each sin and its
relevance to today. What is Paul's conclusion about the condition of all
people? (3:23)

5. Read verses 19-20. What is the purpose of the law? What should sinners'
attitude be toward the holy God? If no one can be declared righteous
before God, who can save us from our sin and death? (Jn 3:16)
