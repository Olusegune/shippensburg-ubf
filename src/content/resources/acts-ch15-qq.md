THE COUNCIL AT JERUSALEM

Acts 15:1-35
Key Verse: 15:11

1. What problem did certain people from Judea create in Antioch? (1) How
did Paul and Barnabas respond? (2) Why? What did the church decide to do?

2. Read verses 3-5. Describe their trip to Jerusalem. How did they use this
opportunity? What did they do upon reaching Jerusalem? What was the issue
raised by some believers who were strict Jews? Why?

3. What did the apostles and elders do? (6-7) After much discussion, who
got up to give the decisive speech? Why was he qualified to speak about
this matter?

4. Read verses 8-11. What was Peter's clear stand? What was the basis of
his conviction? What is necessary for salvation? Why is this such an
important issue?

5. What was Barnabas' and Paul's testimony? (12) Who was James and what was
the main point of his decision? (13-21) On what did he base this? What
several rules did he say that Gentile believers should follow? Why?

6. Read verses 22-35. Who was sent from Jerusalem to Antioch to deliver the
Council's decision? What was the content of the letter? What was the
response in Antioch? How did this event strengthen the church? What is
the significance of this decision to the church through the ages and to
us?
