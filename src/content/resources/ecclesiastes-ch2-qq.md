GOD'S GIFT TO THOSE WHO PLEASE HIM

Ecclesiastes 2:1-26
Key Verse: 2:26

1. What did Solomon discover about laughter and pleasure-two things people
pursue for happiness? (1-3)

2. What did he include in the list of his projects? (4-8) How did his work
give delights in his heart? (9-10) What was the verdict on his
accomplishments? (11) Why did he omit the greatest project in his list?
(1Ki 6:1-38)

3. How did Solomon see the value of wisdom? (12-13) Still, why did he
conclude that both wisdom and folly are meaningless? (15-16)

4. Why did Solomon hate life and all the things? (17-18) Why did his heart
begin to despair over all his toilsome labor under the sun? (19-23)

5. In the midst of meaningless pursuits in life, what did he see from the
hand of God? (24-25) Contrast the life of those who please God with that
of the sinner. (26)
