A REMNANT CHOSEN BY GRACE

Romans 11:1-16
Key Verse: 11:5

1. How was Paul himself evidence that God had not rejected Israel? (1b)
What does it mean that God "foreknew"? (2a; 8:29; 1Pe 1:2) What had
Elijah said, and what was God's answer? (2b-4; cf. 1Ki 19:14,18) How did
Paul apply this? (5)

2. What does it mean that there is a "remnant"? (9:27) Why is it only by
grace? (6; Eph 1:5-6; 2:8-9) What do you learn from God who works through
a remnant chosen by his grace?

3. What were the people of Israel seeking so earnestly, why did they fail,
and what can we learn from this about God? (7-10; 9:18,31)

4. What purpose did Paul see in Israel's rejection of Jesus? (11) What hope
for Israel did he also see? (12) Why did he make so much of his Gentile
ministry? (13-14)

5. What blessing did the world receive through Israel's rejection, and what
does this mean? (15; Ro 5:10; 2Co 5:18-20) How did Paul see Israel? (16)
