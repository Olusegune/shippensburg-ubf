MOSES' DECISION OF FAITH

Hebrews 11:23-28
Key Verse: 11:24-25

"By faith Moses, when he had grown up, refused to be known as the  son
of Pharaoh's daughter.  He chose  to  be  mistreated  along  with  the
people of God rather than to enjoy the pleasures of sin  for  a  short
time."

1.    What was the situation of the Israelites at the time of Moses'  birth?
(Ex 1)  What did Moses' parents do by faith?  (23)   How  did  they  see
their child with the eyes of faith?  Why were they  not  afraid  of  the
king's edict?  Who they fear instead? (Ac 7:20; Lk 12:4-5)

2.    As a prince of Egypt, what special privileges could Moses  enjoy?  (Ac
7:22)  What decision of faith did he make? (24)

3.    What was the price he had to pay because of the  decision?   In  spite
of that, why did Moses choose to suffer with the people of God?  (25-26)
Why did he regard disgrace for the sake of Christ as  of  greater  value
than the treasures of Egypt?

4.    How did Moses leave Egypt? (27)  What did Moses do in  order  to  save
the firstborn of Israel from God's  destroyer?  (28)   Think  about  the
power of the blood.
