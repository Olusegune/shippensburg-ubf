I HAD TO BE IN MY FATHER'S HOUSE

Luke 2:41-52
Key Verse: 2:49

1.    Read verses 41-42. What did the Passover Feast celebrate? Why is it
important to remember and celebrate what God has done? Remember God's
purpose in redeeming his people from slavery (Ex 19:4-6).

2.    How often did Jesus' parents celebrate the Passover in Jerusalem?
What does this show about this family? When did they take Jesus? What is
special about his being twelve years old?

3.    Read verses 43-47. When did Jesus' parents miss him? How did they
find him? How must they have felt? Where was Jesus and what was he doing?
Why were the people amazed at him?

4.    Read verses 48-50. Why were his parents astonished? How and why did
his mother rebuke him? How did the boy Jesus respond to his mother? What
does this answer mean? (Mt 6:9) Why do we need two fathers?

5.    Describe Jesus' attitude in Bible study (46-47). What does his
attitude show about his inner desires? (See Dt 6:5) What does it show
about him that he "had to be" in his Father's house? (KJV: "...about my
Father's business")

6.    Read verses 51-52. How was Jesus different from many teenagers today?
Why is it important for young people to learn obedience? What can we
learn here about Jesus' boyhood? In what ways is he an example for young
people?
