REIGN WITH CHRIST A 1,000 YEARS

Revelation 20:1-15
Key Verse: 20:4

I saw thrones on which were seated those who had been given authority
to judge. And I saw the souls of those who had been beheaded because
of their testimony for Jesus and because of the word of God. They had
not worshiped the beast or his image and had not received his mark on
their foreheads or their hands. They came to life and reigned with
Christ a thousand years.

1. What did John see coming down from heaven? (1) What did the angel do to
Satan? (2-3) Why did the angel do this to Satan?

2. What souls did John see sitting on thrones? (4) With whom did they
reign? (4) What is this reigning with Christ called? Why are those who
have a part considered blessed and holy? (5-6)

3. What happens to Satan when the thousand years are over? (7) What will he
do and what does this show about him? (8-9) What will happen before they
succeed? (9) What will finally happen to the devil? (10) Why does God
want us to know this?

4. What does John see next? What about the earth and the heaven? (11) Who
does John see standing before the throne? What was opened (12) How were
the dead judged? (12)

5. Who had given up the dead? (13) What is the lake of fire called? Why?
Who was cast into it? (14-15) How should the ultimate judgment of evil
and the reward of faithfulness affect the way we live now?
