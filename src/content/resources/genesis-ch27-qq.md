GOD MEETS JACOB AT BETHEL

Genesis 27-28
Key Verse: 28:20-22

* JACOB GETS THE BLESSINGS (27:1-28:22)

1.  How did Isaac instruct Esau and why? (1-4) (Why did Isaac think  he  was
going to die soon? (1) His death is recorded in 35:28,29)

2.  What did Rebekah tell Jacob to do and why? (5-10)

3.  Why did Jacob hesitate? How did Rebekah teach him  the  life  and  death
importance of getting the blessing? What did she risk? What can we  learn
from her?

4.  How did Jacob and Rebekah deceive Isaac? What  was  not  so  good  about
their method? (14-25a)

5.  How did Isaac bless Jacob? What in this blessing would  make  Esau  mad?
(25b-29)

6.  What did Esau do and say when he found that Jacob had deceived him?  How
did Isaac bless him? Describe Esau. (see 27:41; 28:6-9) Why  was  he  not
fitted to be the covenant son? (Remember 25:32-34)

7.  Why did Rebekah decide to send Jacob away? Why was it hard for her?  How
did she persuade Isaac to send him away? (27:41-28:2)

* JACOB'S VOW (28:3-22)

8.  What were Isaac's instructions and blessing to  Jacob  as  he  sent  him
away from home? Where was he going?

9.  Think about how "mommy boy" Jacob felt that first night away from  home.
(28:10,11)

10.  Describe his dream. How did God reveal himself to Jacob? What  promises
did God make to him? What is the  significance  of  God's  revelation  to
Jacob?

11.  How did Jacob respond to the dream? What did he do the next morning?
How does this show his sense of history? What was his basic attitude
toward God?

12.  Describe Jacob's vow. What does it show about  his  condition  and  his
inner life? What does it show about his faith?  Why  was  this  event  so
important in Jacob's life? Have you ever made a "Jacob's vow"? If so, how
has it been important to your life? If not, can you make one now?
