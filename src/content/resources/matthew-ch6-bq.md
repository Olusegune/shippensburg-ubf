SEEK FIRST HIS KINGDOM

Matthew 6:19-34
Key Verse: 6:33

"But seek first his kingdom and his righteousness, and all these things
will be given to you as well."

1. Read verses 19-21. What are treasures on earth and how do we store them
up? What are treasures in heaven and how do we store them up? Why must we
store up treasures in heaven?

2. What does it mean that the eye is the lamp of the body? (22,23) What are
good eyes? Bad eyes? How should we see the world? What happens if we do
not see the world from God's point of view?

3. Read verse 24. How does this verse fit the flow of the previous verses?
Who are the two masters between which we must choose? Why can't we serve
both?

4. Read verses 25-27. Why should we not worry about the things that seem
very necessary to our lives? How does God show his faithfulness to
provide for the necessities of those who depend only on him? What cannot
be accomplished by worrying? What are the ill effects of worry?

5. What do most people worry about that we should not worry about? (28-30)
How does God dress with beautiful apparel the flowers and grass? What is
the difference in what is temporal and what is eternal? Why do people
worry? (30b)

6. Read verses 31-34. What are some basic things that both pagans--the
unbelieving world--and Christians need? How do the pagans seek? How must
Christians seek?

7. What does it mean to seek first God's kingdom and his righteousness? How
does this affect the way we live? What does it mean to not worry about
tomorrow?
