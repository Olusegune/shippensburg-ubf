TO SEEK AND TO SAVE THE LOST

Luke 19:1-10
Key Verse: 19:10

1. Read verses 1-4. Where was Jesus going? Who was Zacchaeus? What kind of
man was he? Why did he want to see Jesus? What did he do in order to see
Jesus? What does this tell us about him?

2. Read verses 5-6. What did Jesus do when he reached the spot where
Zacchaeus was sitting in the tree? What did he tell Zacchaeus to do? What
did he invite himself to do? How did Zacchaeus respond? What does this
show about him?

3. Read verse 7. How did the townspeople respond to Jesus' plan to go to
the home of Zacchaeus? Why did they grumble? What does this tell us about
Jesus? About the townspeople?

4. Read verse 8. When Jesus came to his house, what new decision did
Zacchaeus make? Why?

5. Read verses 9-10. How did Jesus respond to Zacchaeus' repentance? Why
did Jesus call him a son of Abraham? What does Jesus teach about himself?
What does this mean to us?
