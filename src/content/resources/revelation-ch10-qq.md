eat the sweEt word of god and prophesy

Revelation 10:1-11:19
Key Verse: 10:9

So I went to the angel and asked him to give me the little scroll. He
said to me, "Take it and eat it. It will turn your stomach sour, but in
your mouth it will be as sweet as honey."

1. Describe the mighty angel that came down from heaven. (1-2) What
happened when the angel shouted like the roar of a lion? (3) What did
John start to do? What was he told and why? (4)

2. What did the angel swear by God? (5-7) What is the significance of the
mystery of God that will be revealed without delay?

3. Read verses 8-10. What is the little scroll? What does it mean to "eat"
the scroll? When has the gospel tasted like honey or turned your stomach
sour?

4. What does it mean to "prophesy"? (11) What shows that witness is not
generated out of human duty but starts in heaven? (10:1) Why is this
important for us to know?

5. What was John told to measure and what to leave out? (11:1-2) Chapter 11
describes two visions: the measuring of the temple and the two witnesses.
What shows that this chapter is really about the witness of God's people
as a whole?

6. Who are the two witnesses and what happens to them? Why? (3-4) What will
happen to those who try to harm them? (5) What power do the witnesses
have? (6)

7. What will happen to the witnesses when they finish their testimony? (7-
8) How will those on the earth react to their deaths? Why? (9-10) What
happened to the witnesses after three and a half days? (11-12) What
happened on the earth in that same hour? (13)

8. What happened when the seventh trumpet was blown? (15) What did the
twenty-four elders do? (16-17) As expressed in their praise, what had
come? (18) What was opened in heaven and what was in it? (19)

9. What do you learn in these chapters about our calling to be God's
witnesses?
