JESUS AT THE TEMPLE

Matthew 21:12-17
Key Verse: 21:13

"It is written," he said to them, "'My house will be called a house of
prayer,' but you are making it a 'den of robbers.'"

1. Read verse 12. When Jesus entered Jerusalem, where did he go and what
did he do? What does this reveal about Jesus? What was the temple's
importance? (What do you know about its contents, purpose and history?
See 1Ch 22:8-10a.)

2. Read verse 13. What did Jesus say to those in the temple? Why did the
religious leaders make the temple like a marketplace or a den of robbers?

3. What did Jesus mean by "my house will be called a house of prayer"? What
is prayer? Think about the place of listening in prayer (Mk 1:35; Ac 16:7-
10).

4. What does Mark 11:17 tell us about prayer topics we should have? (See
also Mt 6:9-15; 1Ti 2:1-4)

5. Read verses 14-17. How does Jesus reveal his compassion? How did the
children's response differ from that of the religious leaders? What can
we learn here? What did Jesus say and do?
