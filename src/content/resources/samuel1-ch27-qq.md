MAKING WRONG DECISIONS OUT OF FEAR

1 Samuel 27:1-28:25
Key Verses: 28:6-7

1. What motivated David to go to Gath? (27:1-4) How did he come to live in
Ziklag? (27:5-7)

2. What was his awkward situation there, and how did he handle it for the
time being? (27:8-12) What was wrong with this? Think of a situation in
which you are tempted to lie. What alternatives do you have?

3. Notice Achish's words: "He will be my servant forever" (27:12). Compare
this with God's will for David. (28:17) As a result, what trap did he
fall into? (28:1-2)

4. What was Saul's desperate situation? (28:3-5) What did he do, and how
did the LORD respond? (28:6) When does God refuse to answer man's
request?

5. To what did Saul resort? (28:7-8; Lev 20:6) What does this show about
him? How did he persuade the woman to summon the spirit of Samuel? (28:9-
14)

6. What judgment did Samuel pronounce? (28:15-19) How did Saul respond?
(28:20-25) How did Saul's consultation with the witch affect him (23-25)
