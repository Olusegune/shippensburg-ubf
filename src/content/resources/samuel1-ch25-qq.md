THE LORD REWARDS US FOR OUR FAITHFULNESS

1 Samuel 25:1-26:25
Key Verses: 26:23-24

1. After Samuel's death, where did David go? (1; see map) How are Nabal and
Abigail described? (2-3) What did David ask of Nabal? (4-8) How did he
respond? (9-11) How did David react to this news? (12-13)

2. At this critical moment, what did Abigail do? (14-22) How did she help
David turn to the Lord and not shed blood? (23-31) How could she have
such wisdom? (Pr 9:10; 31:30)

3. What did David realize through her advice? (32-34) How did he bless her?
(35) How did God deal with Nabal? (36-38) What did David do? (39-44)

4. How did Saul begin to pursue David again? (1-3a) How did David respond,
and whom did he take with him? (3b-6) What did Abishai ask David to let
him do? (7-8) What did David teach him? (9-11a) What conviction did David
have? How did this conviction help him to be patient? (Ps 54:4)

5. What did David take? (11b-12) How did he rebuke Abner? (13-16) How did
he appeal his cause to Saul? (17-20) What did Saul realize and promise?
(21)

6. What did David believe about God, and how did he base his life and
future on this faith? (22-24) How did Saul bless him? (25)
