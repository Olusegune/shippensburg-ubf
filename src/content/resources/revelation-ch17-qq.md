FALLEN IS BABYLON THE GREAT

Revelation 17-18
Key Verse: 17:14

"They will make war against the Lamb, but the Lamb will overcome them
because he is Lord of lords and King of kings--and with him will be
his called, chosen and faithful followers."

1. What did the angel tell John he would be shown? (1) How does the angel
describe this woman? (2) Now carefully describe the woman as seen by John
(3-6a).

2. What was John's reaction to seeing the woman? (6b) What did the angel
offer to tell John? (7) How is the beast described? (8) What do the seven
heads of the beast represent? (9-10) What is said of the seven kings?
(10) What is said of the beast that once was and now is not? (11)

3. What do the ten horns represent? (12) What else is said of the ten kings
and the beast? (13-14) Why will the Lamb overcome the beast and the ten
kings? (14)

4. What do the waters upon which the prostitute sits represent? (15) What
will the ten horns (kings) do to the prostitute? (16) Why will they do
this? (17) What does this teach us about God? How is the woman finally
described? (18) Who do you think this great prostitute symbolizes?

5. Who proclaims the fall of Babylon? (18:1-2) What does the angel say
concerning the nations, kings and merchants in regards to Babylon? (3)

6. What does a voice from heaven implore the people of God? Why? (4-5) To
what degree will Babylon be judged? (6-7) What plagues will come to her
in one day? Her ultimate end? (8)

7. Who is the first group that mourns the fall of Babylon? (9) What will
they say as they see the smoke of her burning from a distance? (10) Who
is the second group that mourns the fall of Babylon? Why will they weep
and mourn? (11-14) What will they say as they stand far off, weeping and
mourning? (15-17a)

8. Who is the third group that mourns the fall of Babylon? (17b) What do
they say as they see the smoke of her burning? (18-19) Who is told to
rejoice over the fall of Babylon? Why? (20)

9     What did a mighty angel do and say? (21) What did the angel say would
not be seen or heard in Babylon anymore? (22-23a) What two reasons are
given for her downfall? (23a-24) What can you learn from this vision of
the fall of Babylon?
