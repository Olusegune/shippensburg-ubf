FOLLOW GOD'S WORD SO THAT YOU MAY LIVE

Deuteronomy 1:1-4:14
Key Verse: 4:1

I. Rebellion Against the Lord (1:1-46)

1. When and where did Moses speak these words to all Israel? (1-5) What had
the Lord said to them at Horeb? (6-8) Which land did he tell them to take
possession of? Why did Moses tell them to choose leaders over them? (9-
18) What were the qualifications of the leaders? What was their mission
and why was it important for them to act as impartial judges?

2. What did Moses say to Israel in Kadesh Barnea? (19-21) Why did they
rebel against the command of the Lord upon hearing the spies' report? How
did Moses encourage them? (29-33)

3. How did God discipline Israel who did not trust him? (34-37) Only who
would enter the promised land and why? (38-39) When the Israelites
belatedly decided to go up and fight, what did the Lord say and what was
the results of their arrogant decision? (40-46)

II. Desert Training (2:1-3:11)

4. How did God train Israel and what new direction did he give them? (2:1-
3) Why did the Lord tell them not to harass the Edomites and the
Moabites? (4-12) What happened to the entire generation of fighting men?
Why? (13-15)

5. What did the Lord say to Israel concerning the Ammonites and Amorites?
(16-37) What was their first taste of war and victory? What did they
learn? How could they defeat the strong army of Bashan in their fortified
cities? (3:1-11)

III. Then You Will Live (3:12-4:14)

6. Before the Israelites crossed the Jordan, how did Moses distribute the
land they had taken over? On what condition? (3:12-20) What two things
did Moses tell Joshua to remember? (21-22) What did Moses plead with the
Lord and what was his answer? (23-29)

7. What did Moses say to Israel concerning God's word? (4:1,2,5,6) How does
God bless those who hear and follow his word carefully? (1b,5,6) How
could Israel become a "great" nation? (6b-8)

8. Why did God tell Israel to assemble at Horeb? (9-10)  How did he declare
to them his covenant, the Ten Commandments? (11-14) What can we learn
from God who appeared to them as only a voice?
