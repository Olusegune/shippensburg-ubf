Hold firmly to the trustworthy message

Titus 1:1-16
Key Verse: 1:5

1. Read verses 1-3. How does Paul introduce himself? What are three values
that define his identity as a servant of God and an apostle of Jesus
Christ? (1,2) Think about God's divine calling upon the apostle Paul. (3)

2. Read verses 4-5. To whom did Paul write this letter? What was his
relationship with Paul? (2Co 2:12-13, 7:5-7,13-16, 8:16-17,23, 12:18).
What was the reason he left Titus in Crete and what was his specific
direction for Titus? (5)

3. Read verses 6-9. What guidelines did Paul give Titus in appointing
elders? What does 'blameless' mean? (Ge 6:9, Job 1:8) What does it mean
to be hospitable? (8)  What other qualities does Paul mention?

4. Read verses 10-14. How does Paul characterize the circumcision group?
(10; Gal 5:11,12) What was their motive in teaching disruptive doctrines?
Why and how must they be silenced? (11) What was the bad reputation the
Cretans had? (12) What did Titus have to do to help them?

5. Read verses 15-16. Discuss their spiritual problems. What was their view
towards others? (15) What is God's view towards them? (16)
