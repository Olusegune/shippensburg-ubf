COME, LET US RETURN TO THE LORD

Hosea 4:1-6:3
Key Verse: 6:3

"Let us acknowledge the LORD; let us press on to acknowledge him. As
surely as the sun rises, he will appear; he will come to us like the
winter rains, like the spring rains that water the earth."

* WHEN THERE IS NO LOVE OF gOD (4:1-19)

1. What specific charges did God bring against the Israelites? (1-2) What
is the destiny of a godless land? (3) Why do we have to "hear the word of
God"?

2. Why couldn't the Israelites accuse one another? (4-9) What does the
phrase "Like people, like priests" tell us about the sins of the
religious leaders? What happens when God's servants neglect the word of
God?

3. How extensive was the idolatry in the culture of the northern Israel
(Ephraim)? (10-19) What warning did God send to the people of the
southern kingdom Judah? (15) To us?

* "THEY WILL EARNESTLY SEEK ME" (5:1-6:3)

4. What was the result of persisting in spiritual idolatry for a long time?
(1-12) What does it mean "Their deeds do not permit them to return to
their God"? (4) What must we do to avoid persistent sin that hardens our
hearts?

5. Why did Israel turn to Assyria for help and what was the result? (13-15;
2Ki 15:19-20, 17:3-4) How was Israel like Hosea's wife? What was God's
deep motive in punishing his people? (15)

6. Read verse 6:1. What can you learn about God's love who injures his
people so that he can really heal them? What should be our attitude in
times of God's discipline? (2-3) How can we return to God and press on to
acknowledge him?
