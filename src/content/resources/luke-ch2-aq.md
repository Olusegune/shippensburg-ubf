A SAVIOR HAS BEEN BORN TO YOU

Luke 2:1-14
Key Verse: 2:10-11

1. Who was Caesar Augustus and how does this passage reveal his power and
influence? What was the purpose of his decree? How did it affect the
Roman world? How did it affect Mary and Joseph?

2. Describe the circumstances of Jesus' birth. What does this reveal about
the atmosphere of society at that time? Why did God send his one and only
Son into such humble circumstances?

3. Compare and contrast the baby in the manger with Caesar Augustus. What
can you learn here about God's history and human history?

4. Who were the first ones to learn about Jesus' birth? What were they
doing? What was their first reaction to the angel? What does this event
show us about the shepherds?

5. Look at the angel's message. Why is Jesus' birth good news of great joy?
What does "for all the people" mean? What was the sign God gave the
shepherds? What is significant about this sign?

6. What did the choir of angels add to the meaning of Jesus' birth? Think
about why Jesus' birth is glory to God. Think about why the baby Jesus in
the manger gives peace to men.
