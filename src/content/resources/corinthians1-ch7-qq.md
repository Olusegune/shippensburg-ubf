CHRISTIAN MARRIAGE

1 Corinthians 7:1-40
Key Verses: 7:10,11

1. Read verses 1-6. What does Paul teach us about marriage? (1,2) What is
the meaning of marriage established by God? (Ge 2:15-25) What are the
principles we should remember for a marital relationship? (3-6) Think
about why many marriages today fail.

2. Read verses 7-9. What does Paul say about staying unmarried? Why? Read
verses 10-11. What is God's command on marital faithfulness and divorce?
(Mal 2:16? Mt 19:3-6) Read verses 12-16. What is Paul's teaching for
those whose spouse is an unbeliever?

3. Read verses 17-24. What is the rule Paul lays down for all believers?
What does it mean to "remain in the situation they were in when God
called them"? (20)

4. Read verses 25-35. Why should we not consider marriage or even family to
be our ultimate goal in life? (25-31) What is one advantage in being
single? (32-35) How can we have an undivided devotion to the Lord?

5. Read verses 36-40. What is Paul's advice for those who are engaged? (36-
38) What about widows? (39-40) How would you summarize the biblical view
of marriage?
