DAVID BRINGS THE ARK OF GOD TO JERUSALEM

2 Samuel 5:6-6:23
Key Verses: 6:14-15

1. Look at 5:6-10. What did the Jebusites say to David and why? (Jdg 1:21)
How did their pride and insolence animate David? How did he find a way to
capture the fortress? (7,10) What was the significance of the capture of
Jerusalem, the City of David?

2. Read verses 11-12. What did David know? What does this realization show
us about David and his kingdom's character? How did his family multiply?
(13-14)

3. Read verses 17-25. What can we learn from David who repeatedly inquired
of the Lord in the midst of Philistine attacks? (19,23,25) What did this
reveal about his personal relationship with the Lord?

4. Look at 6:1-11. What was David's purpose in bringing the ark to
Jerusalem? (1Ch 13:3; Ps 132:4-5,7,13-14) Why and how did God display his
holiness? What lessons did David learn from the failure? (6:9; 1Ch 15:2)

5. Look at verses 12-23. When his second attempt was a success, how did he
lead his people to worship and praise God? What was Michal's reaction?
How did she not understand his joy?

6. What can we learn here about being a spiritual leader?
