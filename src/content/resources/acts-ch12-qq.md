THE CHURCH PRAYS EARNESTLY FOR PETER

Acts 12:1-25
Key Verse: 12:5

1. Read verses 1-5. When and where did these events take place? Why and how
did King Herod intensify his persecution of the church? What was Herod's
intention and what did the believers do?

2. Read verses 6-11. What was Peter's situation in prison? What does this
show about him and about Herod? How did God rescue Peter? When and how
did he realize what was happening?

3. Read verses 12-17. Where did Peter go? What were the believers doing?
What happened when Peter arrived? What does this event show about the
church, about God, and about prayer?

4. Read verses 18-23. What was Herod's reaction to Peter's escape? Where
did Herod go? With whom did he have an audience? What did the people of
Tyre and Sidon want? What happened to Herod? Why? Contrast Herod and
Peter.

5. Read verses 24-25. What do these verses tell us about the spread of the
word of God and of the Christian church? (6:7; 9:31; 16:5; 19:20; 28:31)
What can we learn from this?
