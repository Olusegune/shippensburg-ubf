AT THE PLACE GOD WILL CHOOSE

Deuteronomy 12:1-13:18
Key Verse: 12:18

1. After entering the Promised Land, how decisively should Israel deal with
the Canaaite idols and their places of worship? (1-3) How should they
worship the Lord God? (4-14) Why only at the place the Lord God will
choose? What can you learn about worship?

2. What kind of freedom did the Israelites have in eating meat (in terms of
place and quanity)? (15,20-22) What were some restrictions? (16-18,26-27)
Why? (25,28) How were they to treat the Levites and why? (12,19)

3. What did Moses tell his people to be careful about, once they settled in
the land of Canaan? (29-30) How did the Canaanites worship their gods?
(31) What should God's people do instead of paying attention to idols and
idol worship? (32)

4. When a prophet or a dreamer tempts God's people to follow and worship
other gods, what should they do and why? (13:1-5) What if the temptation
comes from one's own friends or family members, including brother, son,
daughter, or wife? (6-11) Why should they deal with them so decisively?
(5b,11)

5. What must be done to a town that turns against the Lord to worship other
gods? How will the Lord bless his people when they purge idol worship
from their community and obey the Lord by keeping all his commands?
