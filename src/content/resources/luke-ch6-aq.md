JESUS IS LORD OF THE SABBATH

Luke 6:1-11
Key Verse: 6:5

1. Read verses 1-5. Where were Jesus and his disciples one Sabbath day? Why
and how did the Pharisees criticize the disciples? Why do you think they
were watching closely Jesus and his disciples? (7)

2. How did Jesus respond to the Pharisees' criticism and expose their poor
Bible study? How and why did David violate the religious law? What should
the Pharisees have learned from him?

3. What does it mean that "the Son of Man is Lord of the Sabbath"?

4. Read verses 6-11. When Jesus went to the synagogue on another Sabbath,
who was there? Why was the man's shriveled right hand a serious life
problem for him? (Put yourself in his place.)

5. How does the Pharisees' attitude toward this man and toward Jesus reveal
their serious problem? What did Jesus do to challenge them and expose
their evilness?

6. What command did Jesus give to test and build the man's faith? Why might
it be hard for him to obey Jesus? When he obeyed, what happened? What can
we learn from Jesus?
