THE PARABLE OF THE TENANTS

Matthew 21:33-46
Key Verse: 21:33

"Listen to another parable: There was a landowner who planted a
vineyard. He put a wall around it, dug a winepress in it and built a
watchtower. Then he rented the vineyard to some farmers and went away
on a journey."

1. What is the time and place of this parable (21:1-11, 23)?  Who was
listening to him?  What is the overall tone of this parable?

2. Read verse 33-34. Who might the landowner represent? (See Isaiah 40:11-
13) How did he prepare his vineyard? What did he expect of the tenants?
In what sense are we the stewards of God's world? (Eph 2:10; Ge 1:28)

3. Read verses 35-36. What happened when the servants came to collect the
fruit? (35,36) Who do the servants represent? Why did the tenants behave
like this?

4. Read verses 37-39. Why did the owner send his son? What did the tenants
think and do when they saw the son? Why do you think Jesus depicted the
killing of the son in this parable?

5. Read verses 40-46. What will the owner do? Read verse 43.  Think about
Jesus' purpose in telling this parable.  How should his listeners
respond?  How did some listeners' reaction prove that they were the
tenants in the parable? (45,46)  What deep principle is revealed in this?
(42)
