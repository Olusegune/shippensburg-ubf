JESUS WAS CRUCIFIED

John 19:16b-42

Key Verse: 19:30

1. Read verses 16b-18. In what ways did Jesus suffer excruciating pain and
terrible shame? What did his crucifixion mean from God's point of view?
(Jn 1:29; Jn 20:31)

2. Read verses 19-24. What was Pilate's testimony? What does it mean that
he is "Jesus of Nazareth"? "King of the Jews?" How did the soldiers
reveal fallen man's mentality? How did John view their actions? (24; Ps
22:18)

3. Read verses 25-27. Who were these sorrowful women? (Lk 8:2,3) Think
about his mother's pain (Lk 2:35) What did he say to his mother and to
John? Why?

4. Read verses 28-30. What does Jesus' cry, "I am thirsty" reveal about
him? What does he mean by "It is finished"? Why was Jesus' death not a
tragedy? (Rev 1:8; Jn 1:1) What does this mean to us that Jesus finished
his mission?

5. Read verses 31-37. Why did the soldiers break the legs of the other two
men? Why not Jesus' legs? What did the flow of blood and water mean? How
did John the eyewitness see these events? (35-37)

6. What is the significance of Jesus' burial by two prominent Sanhedrin
members? How were they changed by Jesus' death? What does the death of
Jesus mean to you?
