"I STAND AT THE DOOR AND KNOCK"

Revelation 3:1-22
Key Verse: 3:20

"Here I am! I stand at the door and knock. If anyone hears my voice and
opens the door, I will come in and eat with him, and he with me."

1. What was the problem in the Sardis church? How did its reputation not
match with the reality? Why was this so serious?

2. What did the Lord urge the church to do? (2-3) What are his warning and
promise? (3b-6) What does it mean to be "dressed in white"?

3. What does it mean Jesus opens doors and closes them? (7) How was the
church of Philadelphia faithful to Christ? (8) Describe a time when you
have struggled to remain faithful to God. What helped you persevere in
your faithfulness?

4. What rewards did Jesus promise to the Philadelphians? (9-13) What can we
learn from this?

5. Laodicea is the only church about which Jesus had nothing good to say.
Why would Christ rather us be hot or cold than lukewarm? What might have
caused the church to become lukewarm in their faith? In what sense might
God call you "lukewarm"?

6. Why did Christ call the Laodiceans wretched, miserable, poor, blind, and
naked if they were wealthy? What would it take for this church to get out
of the miserable state? (17-18)

7. How does Jesus demonstrate his love for this church? (19-22) What does
it mean to open the door for Jesus? Why does he want us to have
fellowship with him?
