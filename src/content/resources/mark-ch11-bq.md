"HAVE FAITH IN GOD"

Mark 11:20-25
Key Verse: 11:22

"Have faith in God," Jesus answered.

1. Read verses 20-21. As Jesus and his disciples walked along, what did
Peter notice? Why was he surprised? How is this event connected to the
cleansing of the temple?

2. Read verses 22-23. How did Jesus respond? Why did he command them to
have faith in God? What did Jesus teach them about faith and power?

3. What does it mean to have faith in God? Why must faith challenge human
limits?

4. Read verse 24. What is the connection between faith and prayer? Why must
one who has faith pray? Why must one who prays have faith? Why must we
overcome doubt? How can we?

5. Read verse 25. What hinders prayer? Why? What can we do about those who
have hurt us with real or imagined wrongs? Why did Jesus make forgiveness
a condition for receiving God's forgiveness?
