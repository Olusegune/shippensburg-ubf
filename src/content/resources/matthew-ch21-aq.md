SEE, YOUR KING COMES TO YOU

Matthew 21:1-11
Key Verse: 21:5

Say to the Daughter of Zion, "See, your king comes to you, gentle and
riding on a donkey, on a colt, the foal of a donkey."

1. Read verses 1-3. What instructions did he give two disciples as they
neared Jerusalem? What were they to say to anyone who raised a question?
How does this underscore the lordship of Christ? What does this mean to
you?

2. Why was Jerusalem such an important place in God's history? (Isa 2:3)
What was Jesus anticipating in Jerusalem? (20:17-19)

3. Read verses 4-5 and Zechariah 9:9-10. What does this prophecy tell us
about the meaning of Jesus' Triumphal Entry? Why "triumphal"? About the
nature of Jesus' kingship?

4. Read verses 6-11. Why might it be hard for the disciples to obey Jesus'
command? What did Jesus do? How did the crowd welcome him? What is the
meaning of the crowd's shout of welcome?

5. What question did his entrance to Jerusalem in this way raise? (10-11)
What must we learn about Jesus the Messiah from this event? How does this
apply to our life?
