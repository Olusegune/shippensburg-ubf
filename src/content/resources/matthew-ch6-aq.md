"THIS IS HOW YOU SHOULD PRAY"

Matthew 6:1-18
Key Verses: 6:9,10

This, then, is how you should pray: "Our Father in heaven, hallowed be
your name, your kingdom come, your will be done on earth as it is in
heaven."

1. Read verses 1-4. What is an "act of righteousness"? What is the "act of
righteousness" mentioned by Jesus? How should this "act" be done? What
does it mean to live before God and not before men? Why must we?

2. Read verses 5-8. How do hypocrites pray? What reward do those who pray
in this way receive? How does God want us to pray? Why? What should be
our relationship to God?

3. Read verses 9-13. Think about each prayer topic in the Lord's Prayer.
"Our Father in heaven, hallowed be your name." What does this teach us
about who God is? About how we should approach him? What does it mean to
hallow his name?

4. "Your kingdom come, your will be done on earth as it is in heaven." What
does this teach us about God's great vision and heart's desire? About
Jesus' mission and purpose? About the nature of God's kingdom?

5. "Give us today our daily bread." Why "our" bread? Why do we ask for
"daily" bread? Why for "bread"? What does this teach us about our
relationship to God and to our fellow human beings?

6. "Forgive us our debts, as we also have forgiven our debtors." What is
meant by "debts"? (14-15) Why is it so important to forgive and to be
forgiven? Why won't our Father forgive us if we refuse to forgive others?

7. "Lead us not into temptation, but deliver us from the evil one." What
does this temptation have to do with the evil one? Why do we need God's
help and protection in this matter?

8. Read verses 16-18. What are the right way and the wrong way of fasting?
What do these verses teach about living before God?
