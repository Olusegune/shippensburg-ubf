WHAT IS YOUR NAME?

Luke 8:26-39
Key Verse: 8:30

1.    Read verses 26-27. Where did Jesus and his disciples land? What do
you know about this place? Who met them? Describe this man's appearance
and lifestyle. What do his actions reveal about him? How do you think he
came to be possessed by demon?

2.    What did Jesus do when he saw this demon-possessed man? (29) What did
this man do when he saw Jesus? (28) Why did he act like this?

3.    Read verse 30. Why did Jesus ask his name? (Jn 10:3) How did he
reply? What does this mean?

4.    Read verses 31-37. What did the demons beg of Jesus? Why did Jesus
consent? (Jn 10:11) What happened to the pigs? To the man? What was the
result for Jesus? Why? What does Jesus teach about the value of one
person?

5.    Read verses 38-39. What did the man want to do? Why? What did Jesus
tell him to do instead? Why? How did this man show that he remembered
God's grace? Why is this important?
