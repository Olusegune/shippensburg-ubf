I AM THE LORD WHO MAKES YOU HOLY

Leviticus 21,22,24
Key Verse: 22:32

* Laws for priests and high priest (chapter 21)

1. What are the laws for the priests concerning taking part in funeral
ceremony, customs, and marriage? (1-9) Why must the priests be holy?
(4,6,8)

2. What are the laws for the high priest concerning customs, taking part in
a funeral ceremony, and marriage? (10-15) Why are the laws for the high
priest stricter than for the ordinary priests?

3. Among descendants of Aaron, who is not allowed to exercise priestly
office? (16-24) Why not? (23)

* Laws for sacred offerings (chapter 22)

4. How should the priests respect the sacred offerings? (1-16) How can we
eat Jesus' food and how should we treat it? (11, Jn 6:35; 1Co 11:27-29)

5. What kinds of offerings are not acceptable to God? (17-30) How about
your own offering and worship? Notice how many times "I am the LORD, who
makes you holy" is repeated. (21:8,15,23; 22:9,16,32) How does God make
us holy? (31,32; Ex 19:5,6; Jn 17:17)

* The duties of God's people (chapter 24)

6. What is laymen's duty? (1-2) And priests' duties and the meaning of
each? (3-9)

7. What incident happened? (10-11) What was the punishment for blaspheming
God's name? (12-16,23)

8. Read verses 17-22. What is the punishment for killing a human being?
(17,21b) For killing someone's animal? (18,21a) For injuring one's
neighbor? (19-20) What is God's intention in the laws?
