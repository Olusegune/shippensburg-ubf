DAVID BUILDS AN ALTER TO THE LORD

2 Samuel 24:1-25
Key Verse: 24:25

1. What did David do and why? (1-2: 1Ch 21:1) What was wrong with taking a
census? How did Joab respond, and how did David overrule him? (3-4) What
were the census tallies? (5-9)

2. Afterwards, what did David confess? (10) What were the three options the
Lord gave David? Which option did David choose, and what happened? (11-
16) How did David show himself a shepherd? (17)

3. What direction did God give David through Gad? (18-19) How did David
insist on paying for the site and materials for the altar and offering?
(20-25a) What happened? (25b) What is the historical significance of this
site? (1Ch 22:1)

4. What can we learn in chapter 24 about God and about the priestly role of
a shepherd king?
