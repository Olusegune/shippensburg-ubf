JESUS SENDS OUT THE TWELVE

Luke 9:1-9
Key Verse: 9:2

1. Read verses 1-2. When Jesus sent the Twelve out for fieldwork training,
what spiritual equipment did he give them? Why must gospel workers have
the power and authority of Jesus? (Php 4:13)

2. What was the main point of their message? Why is the message of the
kingdom of God important? (Mk 1:15; Lk 11:2) How is healing the sick
related to the message of the kingdom of God?

3. Read verses 3-5. What did he tell them to take for the journey? (3) How
did this teach them to depend on God alone? What are the timeless
principles which Jesus' disciples must practice?

4. How did Jesus teach them to master the circumstances of the mission
field? (4) How did he teach them not to compromise, but to have absolute
faith in the gospel? (5; Mk 16:16) Why is it important to know that the
gospel is absolute?

5. Read verses 6-9. How did the apostles respond? (6) What was the result
of the fieldwork training? How does Herod's response to the proclamation
of the gospel typify the response of proud and unrepentant people?
