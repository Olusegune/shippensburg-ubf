THE FULL ARMOR OF GOD

Ephesians 6:10-24
Key Verse: 6:11

1. Read verses 10-12. What was Paul's encouragement to the Ephesians in
verse 10? How can believers be strong? Why must we be? What are the
things we are called to fight against? What are the things that we should
not? What does this mean?

2. Read verse 13. What can those who put on the armor of God do? Why is
this important? When will this be especially important? How is this
related to our lives of mission?

3. Read verses 14-17. Think about what is the full armor of God. What are
the defense tools that help us stand firm? Why does Paul compare the
truth to a belt, righteousness to a breastplate, the gospel message to a
pair of good shoes, faith to a shield, salvation to a helmet? How is
faith in Jesus like getting dressed?

4. What are the offensive weapons we have been given? Why so few? Why is
the word of God comparable to a sword (Heb 4:12, 2Ti 3:16)? What can we
learn from this about the nature of the spiritual battle we must fight?

5. Read verses 18-20. What was Paul's other source of strength, and what
can be ours? What were the prayer topics Paul suggested for us as
believers? For him as a gospel messenger?

6. Read verses 21-23. Who was Tychicus? What was his mission? What was
Paul's benediction? (23-24) What does he mean by "undying love," and how
is this related to the armor of God?
