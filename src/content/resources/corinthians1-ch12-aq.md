ONE BODY, MANY PARTS

1 Corinthians 12:1-31
Key Verse: 12:12

The body is a unit, though it is made up of many parts; and though all
its parts are many, they form one body. So it is with Christ.

1. Read verses 1-3. What does Paul want Christians in Corinth to know? (1)
When they were pagans, what was their spiritual condition? (2) What is
the evidence that one is led by the Holy Spirit? (3)

2. Read verses 4-11. What are some spiritual gifts of the Holy Spirit? (4-
6, 8-10) What is God's purpose of giving a different kind of spiritual
gift to each person? (7) How does God exercise his sovereignty in giving
spiritual gift? (11)

3. Read verses 12-13. What analogy does Paul use to explain the variety and
unity of the spiritual gifts? (12) How do people of different culture and
status become one? (13)

4. Read verses 14-26. Why can't each part of the body claim its
independence? (14-20) Why can't each part of the body despise other parts
of the body? (21) How did God give equal concern for each member of the
body? (22-26, Ro 12:4-5)

5. Read verses 27-31. What is the relationship between the church and the
individual? (27) What are some offices in the church? (28) What attitude
should each of them have toward one another? (29,30) What gifts should we
eagerly desire? (31) What are the greater gifts?
