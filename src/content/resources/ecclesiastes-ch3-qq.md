A TIME FOR EVERYTHING

Ecclesiastes 3:1-22
Key Verse: 3:1

1. What does it mean that there is a proper time for everything? (1) Why is
it important to discern the right time to do things? (2Co 6:2)

2. Read verses 2-8 and consider the 14 different turns of time in life. How
would you apply them to your own life? How can we discern God's time
versus our own time? (Jn 2:4, 7:6, 17:1)

3. Solomon contrasts time and eternity in verses 9-15. What burdens and
gifts has God given us for our temporary life on earth? What does it mean
he has also set eternity in our hearts? How can we prepare for our
eternity with God? (Heb 11:13-16)

4. Despite the injustice in this world (16), how do we know that there will
be a time of God's judgment, and that it will be righteous judgment? (17;
Ro 2:5-11)

5. If there were no life after death, we would live like animals-to satisfy
our flesh desires (18-21; 2Co 15:32-34). How would you answer the
question, "who can bring them to see what will happen after them?" (22)
