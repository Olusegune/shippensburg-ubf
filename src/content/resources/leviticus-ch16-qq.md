THE DAY OF ATONEMENT

Leviticus 16:1-34
Key Verse: 16:30

1. What was God's warning to Aaron? Why? (1-2; Ex 33:20) When and how
should the high priest enter the Most Holy Place? (3-14) What does this
teach us?

2. Read verses 15-19. How did the high priest make atonement for his
people? What sins? (16,19) How and why did he make atonement for the
Most Holy Place, the Tent of Meeting and the altar of incense?

3. How was the scapegoat chosen? (7-8) How did it carry all the sins of the
Israelites? (20-22) How is it related to Jesus' sacrifice? (Jn 1:29)

4. Read verses 23-28. Why should the serving priests bathe themselves after
service? (24,26,28) Why should they burn the flesh of the sin offerings
outside the camp?

5. Why was the Day of Atonement most important to the Israelites?
(29,30,34) How must they prepare for it? (29,31) What was the most
important task of the high priest on the day? (32,33) How did the Day of
Atonement foreshadow the atonement Jesus completed for us?
