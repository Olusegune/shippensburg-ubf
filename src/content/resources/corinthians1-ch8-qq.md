HOW TO USE OUR FREEDOM

1 Corinthians 8:1-9:27
Key Verse: 9:19

1. What was another problem in the Corinthian church?  (1a)  In  what  sense
love is more important than knowledge? (1b) Who is known  by  God?  (2,3;
1Jn 4:7,8)

2. What knowledge is Paul talking about? (4-6) Why is it alright for
Christians to eat food sacrificed to idols? To whom is it a problem? (7)
What was Paul's view of food? (8)

3. How should we exercise our rights? (9-12) What was Paul's personal
decision? Why? How can we apply this to our life today?

4. Why did Paul mention about his apostleship? (1-2) His rights? (3-6) How
did he defend this from a human point of view? (7-8a) From a Scriptural
point of view? (8b-14)

5. Why didn't Paul use any of these rights? (12,15) Why didn't he boast
about his sacrificial life? (16,17) What did he consider as his reward?
(18)

6. How did Paul use his freedom? (19) What are some examples he gives? (20-
23) Think about Paul who voluntarily became a slave to everyone.

7. Read verses 24-27. Who gets the prize in a race? (24) What is required
of all athletes who want to win the prize? (25) What is required of those
who want to get a crown that will last forever? What spiritual struggle
had Paul fought? (26,27)
