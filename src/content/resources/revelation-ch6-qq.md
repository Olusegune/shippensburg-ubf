THE BEAUTIFUL WRATH OF the lamb

Revelation 6:1-17
Key Verse: 6:9-10

When he opened the fifth seal, I saw under the altar the souls of those
who had been slain because of the word of God and the testimony they had
maintained. They called out in a loud voice, "How long, Sovereign Lord,
holy and true, until you judge the inhabitants of the earth and avenge
our blood?"

1. Where does this vision take place? Summarize the events during the first
four seals (1-8). What do you think each rider represent? What would it
be like to see each of the four horses and riders?

2. What happens when the fifth and sixth seals are opened? (9-17) Which of
the seven seals seem to be "normal" hard times, and which suggest
something more final? (compare 1-8 with 12-14)

3. Notice the words "Come" and "was given" repeated in this passage, as
seals are opened. How do you respond to the fact that these things
happening on earth are part of God's plan? How do you react to the
prediction that there are more martyrdoms to come? (11)

4. Contrast the experience of the saints (9-11) with that of the
unbelievers (15-17) as they anticipate the impending wrath of God. What
evidence, if any, do you see of this contrast among people today?

5. Are you convinced that the wrath of the Lamb is beautiful as well as
terrible? What comforts do you have when you read about the impending
judgment of God?
