TO THE HOPE WE PROFESS

Hebrews 9:1-10:39
Key Verse: 10:23

* THE EARTHLY TABERNACLE AND CHRIST (9:1-10:18)

1. Describe the earthly tabernacle.  (cf. Ex. 25)  What was in the Most
Holy Place?  Why were the stone tablets of the covenant so important?
What did the atonement cover teach about God and sin? (9:1-5)

2. Who could enter the two rooms of the tabernacle?  Who could enter the
inner room?  How often?  For what purpose?  Why could he not enter
without blood?  (6,7)

3. Why was it necessary for the people of Israel to worship at the
tabernacle?  In what respect was this worship inadequate? (7-10)

4. How does Christ fulfill his priestly ministry?  Why is the new covenant
in Jesus' blood better than the old covenant?  (9:11-14)

5. How is a will put into effect?  How is a covenant like a will? (15-22)
How was the old covenant put into effect? (Ex 24)  The new covenant? (Lk
22:17-22)  What does this mean practically to us? (See Jer 31:31)

6. How was Christ's sacrifice fundamentally different from the sacrifices
offered under the old covenant?

7. What does it mean that Christ's sacrifice is "once for all"?

8. What does 10:1-18 teach about the importance of the new covenant and the
effectiveness of Jesus' blood?

* THEREFORE, BROTHERS  (10:19-39)

9. Since we have been bound to Jesus by the new covenant in his blood, what
must we do? (19-26)  (Note "Let us...")

10. Why is it important to persevere in our Christian life and not shrink
back? (26-39)  How can we?  What is the hope we profess?
