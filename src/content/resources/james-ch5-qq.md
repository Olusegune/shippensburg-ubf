PATIENCE IN SUFFERING

James 5:1-20
Key Verse: 5:7

Be patient, then, brothers, until the Lord's coming. See how the
farmer waits for the land to yield its valuable crop and how patient
he is for the autumn and spring rains.

* WARNING TO RICH OPPRESSORS (5:1-6)

1. What three kinds of wealth do the rich have? (2-3) What does James say
these people have to look forward to? (1)

2. What are the abuses the rich committed? (4-6) How do these injustices
happen today? Can you think of an example where you have "failed to pay
the workmen who mowed your fields"? Where you've lived in self-
indulgence? Condemned innocent men? Hoarded wealth? What should be
Christian's attitude toward injustices in the world?

* BE PATIENT (5:7-12)

3. Read verse 7. Why does James compare Christians to farmers? What can the
farmer do while waiting for spring rains, autumn and the crop? What
should we guard against as we wait for the Lord's coming? (8-9)

4. What do you know about Job and his suffering? (Job 1:1-2:13; 42:1-17)
Why does James use the illustration of Job? (10-11)  What other persons
come to mind as an example of patience and perseverance? How does their
example encourage you?

5. What is the point of verse 12? How seriously do you take sincerity and
follow-through in your commitments?

* THE PRAYER OF FAITH (5:13-20)

6. Read verse 13. What should we do when in trouble? When happy? When we
are sick? (14) Think about why prayer, especially united prayer, is so
essential to the life of the church (14-16). Why are confession and
prayer with each other a powerful part of repentance and healing? Is your
prayer as powerful as Elijah's? (17-18) Why or why not?

7. Read verses 19-20. What can you learn about God's greatest concern from
these verses? Why did Elijah pray for no rain first, and then pray for
rain three and a half years later? What should be our foremost prayer
topic?
