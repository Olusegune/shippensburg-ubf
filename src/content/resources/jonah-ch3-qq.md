JONAH OBEYS THE WORD OF GOD

Jonah 3:1-10
Key Verse: 3:3

Jonah obeyed the word of the Lord and went to Nineveh. Now Nineveh was
a very important city-a visit required three days.

1. What was God's command that came to Jonah a second time? (1-2) What can
we learn about God who helped a reluctant missionary?

2. How did Jonah respond to God's command? (3) What do you know about the
city of Nineveh? (3b; 4:11) What does the phrase "On the first day" show
about Jonah's attitude towards his mission? (4a)

3. Nineveh was the capital city of Assyria, one of the most powerful
nations on earth at that time and was known for its military might and
brutality. How would this make it difficult for Jonah to be obedient?

4. What was Jonah's message? (4) How did the Ninevites respond to his
message, "Forty more days and Nineveh will be overturned!"? (5)

5. How did the king of Nineveh react when he heard the message of Jonah? (6-
9) What showed that their repentance was sincere? (7-8) What was the
king's prayer topic? (9)

6. Describe one area in your life where you see a need for repentance.
What actions can you take to demonstrate the sincerity of your
repentance?

7. How did God respond to the Ninevites? Why? What can we learn about God's
concern for all peoples of the world?
