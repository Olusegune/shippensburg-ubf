LIFE IS A GIFT OF GOD

Ecclesiastes 5:1-20
Key Verse: 5:19

1. What are the right attitudes in prayer as we approach God? (1-3) Think
about specifics of the right attitudes (e.g., humility, gratitude, trust,
...) Why are they more important than many words? (Mt 6:7-8)

2. Why do people make a vow to God? (Ge 28:20-22; 1Sa 1:11; Jdg 11:30-31)
Why should we keep our promises to God? (4-6; Nu 30:2) What does it mean
to fear God? (7)

3. How is justice routinely denied in a sinful world? (8-9) Who is the
ultimate authority we must answer to in the end? How meaningless is to
think that wealth brings true happiness? (10-12; 1Ti 6:9-10)

4. How foolish is it to focus only on money and wealth on earth? (13-17;
Job 1:21) What should be our focus? (Mt 6:19-21)

5. How can our life be joyful and satisfying whether we are rich or poor?
(18-19) Are you busy enjoying your life that you don't have time to
consider your days? (20)
