THE FAITH OF ABRAHAM

Hebrews 11:8-22
Key Verse: 11:8

"By faith Abraham, when called to go to a place he would later receive
as his inheritance, obeyed and went, even though he did not know where
he was going."

* FAITH THAT BELIEVES PROMISE (8-16)

1.    How did Abraham obey God when  he  was  called?  (8a)  What  practical
difficulties did he face when he tried to obey God's calling? (8b)  What
did he believe? (Ge 12:2-3)

2.    How did he live in the promised land? (9) What  are  the  difficulties
of living in tents? How could he live such a life?

3.    What was the inheritance that Abraham handed down to Isaac and  Jacob?
What was he looking forward to? (10)

4.    What was Sarah's physical  condition?  (11a)  How  could  Abraham  and
Sarah become parents? Think about how one man Abraham became a source of
blessing for many people. (12)

5.    What was the  attitude  of  the  fathers  of  faith  concerning  God's
promise? (13a) What does it mean that  "they  admitted  that  they  were
aliens and strangers on earth"? (13b)

6.    Why didn't they return to their homeland?  (14-16a)  Why  is  God  not
ashamed to be called their God? (16b) What has God  prepared  for  them?
What can you learn here about what our true hope should be?

* FAITH THAT BLESSES (17-22)

7.    How did God test Abraham? How difficult  was  the  test?  (Ge  22:1-2)
What was Abraham's response? (17-19)

8.    What did Isaac do by faith? (20) What two things did Jacob do when  he
was dying? (21)

9.    What instructions did Joseph give when his  end  was  near?  (22)  How
could he have such a vision?
