GOD MADE JESUS LORD AND MESSIAH

Acts 2:22-47
Key Verse: 2:36

1. Read verse 22. To whom did Peter address? Who did he talk about? Why? Of
what did he remind them? How did they know about these things? What can
we learn about Jesus from Peter's description of his ministry?

2. Read verses 23-24. How did Jesus die? What was God's deliberate plan?
Who did Peter hold responsible for Jesus' death? What did God do to use
the wicked men for his own purpose and glory? Why was it impossible for
death to keep its hold on him?

3. Read verses 25-28. Describe King David's resurrection faith. What was
his source of joy and hope?

4. Read verses 29-35. How did Peter interpret this prophetic psalm of
David? What is his own testimony? How is the Risen Jesus related to the
coming of the Holy Spirit? How does Psalm 110:1 affirm the deity of
Christ and warn his enemies? (34,35)

5. Read verses 36-39. What did Peter declare about who Jesus is? What did
this mean to his listeners? What shall we sinners do? What is God's
promise?

6. Read verses 40-47. How did Peter continue to urge repentance? With what
result? How did the new believers live? How did God continue to work
among them? What was their attitude toward material things? What was
their attitude toward each other? Toward God?
