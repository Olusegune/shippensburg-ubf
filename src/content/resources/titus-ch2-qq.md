IN EVERYTHING SET AN EXAMPLE

Titus 2:1-15
Key Verses: 2:7-8

1. What must Titus teach (1)? What does "sound doctrine" refer to? What
character should the older men have (2)?

2. How should the older women live (3)? How should they train the younger
women (4-5)? Why must the younger women be so trained (5b)?

3. How does Paul teach Titus to help the young men (6)? What must Titus do
in order to help them (7-8a)? Why should he set an example in everything
(8b)?

4. What does Titus teach the slaves (9-10a)? Why (10b)? What does the grace
of God teach us to do (11-13)?

5. Why did Christ give himself for us and redeem us (14)? How should Titus
teach all these (15)?
