THE HIGH PRIEST OF A NEW COVENANT

Hebrews 8:1-9:15
Key Verse: 9:15

* CHRIST'S SUPERIOR HIGH PRIESTLY MINISTRY (8:1-13)

1. Who is our high priest and where does he serve? (8:1,2)

2. Why would Jesus not be a priest if he were still living as a man on
earth? What is the relationship of the earthly priesthood and sanctuary
to the heavenly ones?

3. Why is Jesus' ministry superior to that of the old Aaronic priesthood?
(6) What is a covenant?

4. What were the promises on which the old covenant was founded? (cf. Ex
3:5-11; 19:5,6)

5. Who were the parties of the old covenant and how was it sealed? (Ex
24:8) What did it mean for the people of Israel to have a covenant
relationship with God? (cf. Isa 54:3; Jer 3:1; Hos 9:1)

6. Why was a new covenant needed? What does Jeremiah teach us about the new
covenant? (10-12) What does it mean practically to have a relationship
with God under the new covenant? (cf. Eze 36:25-27; Lk 22:20) Why is the
old covenant obsolete?

* CHRIST, THE HIGH PRIEST OF A NEW COVENANT (9:1-15)

7. According to the old covenant, how was the earthly sanctuary set up?
(9:1-5)

8. When and how could the high priest enter the Most Holy Place? What does
this teach about the inadequacy of the old system, and what does it point
toward? (6-10)

9. When Christ became our high priest, what sanctuary did he enter, and
what sacrifice did he bring? What was the effect of his sacrifice?
(11,12)

10. What does the blood of Christ do for us that the blood of goats and
bulls can't do? (13,14)

11. Why is Christ called the mediator of a new covenant? (15)
