The Song of Moses

Deuteronomy 31:1-34:12
Key Verses: 32:46,47

I. Encouragement and Warning (31)

1. How did Moses encourage the Israelites, who would soon cross the Jordan
to conquer Canaan? (1-6) How did he encourage his successor Joshua? (7,
8) What commands did Moses give to the priests and to the elders of
Israel, after writing down the law? (9-13)

2. After God called Moses and Joshua, how did God predict Israel would
commit sins against him in the future? (14-18) What did God command they
do for Israel? (19-22) Why did God command them to teach the law through
the song?

3. How did God help Joshua? (23) What commands did Moses give to the
Levites after writing the words of the law in a book? (24-30) How does
this law function?

II. The Song of Moses (32)

1. How did Moses praise God's perfect works? (1-4) How had Israel acted
toward God? (5,6)

2. What should Israel remember before God? (7-14) Why did they abandon and
desert God? (15-19) How did God punish unfaithful Israel through the
Gentiles? (20-26)

3. How easily would their enemies misunderstand these things? (27-34) How
would God avenge Israel against the Gentiles? (35, 40-43) When would God
deliver his people? (36-39) What did God want his people to understand
through this? (39)

4. After speaking all the words of this song, what did Moses command
Israel? (44-46) Why was it good for them to obey the words of this song?
(47) What did God say to Moses? (48-52)

III. The Death of Moses (33:1-34:12)

1. How did Moses, a man of God, bless each Israelite tribe before his
death? (33:1-29)

2. Describe how Moses died. (1-8) When Moses laid his hands on Joshua, what
happened to Joshua? (9) How great was Moses? (10-12)
