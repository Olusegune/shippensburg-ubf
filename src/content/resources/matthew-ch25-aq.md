THEREFORE KEEP WATCH

Matthew 25:1-13
Key Verse: 25:13

"Therefore keep watch, because you do not know the day or the hour."

1. Read verse 1. To what does "at that time" refer? Who went out to meet
the bridegroom? What did they take with them? To whom does the bridegroom
refer? Who are the ten virgins?

2. Read verses 2-5. Why were five of them called wise and five called
foolish? What were the ten virgins doing while they waited for the
bridegroom? Why did the oil in their lamps run short?

3. Read verses 6-9. What was the joyful cry that woke them up at midnight?
When they woke up and trimmed their lamps, what request did the foolish
virgins make? What was the answer of the wise virgins? Do you think the
wise virgins were too selfish? Why? What do you think the oil represents?

4. Read verse 10. What happened when the foolish virgins were on their way
to buy oil? What blessing did the wise virgins receive? What does it mean
that "the door was shut?"

5. Read verses 11-12. What happened when the foolish virgins returned? What
reason does the bridegroom give for not opening the door? What does this
reveal about the foolish virgins?

6. Read verse 13. What is Jesus' concluding point? In the context of this
passage what does it mean to keep watch?
