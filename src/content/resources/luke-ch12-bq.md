BUT SEEK HIS KINGDOM

Luke 12:13-34
Key Verse: 12:31

1.    Read verses 13-15. What did a man in the crowd ask Jesus to do? What
warning did Jesus give? What principle did he teach? What is greed and to
where does it lead? (Jas 1:15; Pr 30:8-9)

2.    Read verses 16-21. In Jesus' parable, what did the rich man want to
do with his wealth? (1Co 15:32b) To what does a pleasure-seeking, selfish
lifestyle lead? Why was he called a fool? (Mt 4:4)

3.    Read verses 22-28. What are the things people worry about? Why should
we not worry or be anxious? What should we learn from the lilies? What is
the spiritual problem that lies at the root of anxiety?

4.    Read verses 29-31. How should God's people be different from godless
people? What must we seek? What does it mean to seek his kingdom? What is
God's promise? (Mt 6:33)

5.    Read verses 32-34. How does the reward of our Father contrast with
the rewards the world offers? How can we be rich toward God? (21) Why is
it so important to guard our hearts?
