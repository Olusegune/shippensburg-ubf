THE PROMISE OF ENTERNING HIS REST

Hebrews 3:1-4:13
Key Verse: 4:1

* JESUS IS SUPERIOR TO MOSES (3:1-6)

1. Why did the Israelites revere Moses so highly? In what respects are
Jesus and Moses alike? How are they different, and in what respects is
Jesus greater?

2. What was Moses' role in God's house? What is Jesus' role? What is God's
house which the writer speaks of here?

* DO NOT HAREN YOUR HEARTS (3:7-19)

3. To what event does the quote from Psalm 95:7-11 refer? (Nu 20:1-13)

4. What did the Israelites of Moses' day do that angered God most? How were
they punished? What did it mean that "they shall never enter my rest"?

5. What lesson should we learn from the Israelites in the desert? How can
we hold fast to Christ to the end? What do the words, "as long as it is
called Today" mean?

* TRUE REST FOR THE PEOPLE OF GOD (4:1-13)

6. What promise did God give to the Israelites? What promise do we have in
Jesus? How does the promise to enter God's rest still stand?

7. Why did the Israelites of Moses' time fail to enter the Promised Land?
What is our promised land? How is our position and opportunity similar to
theirs? What should we learn from them?

8. What is the origin of the Sabbath-rest? Why was the Sabbath-rest taken
away from man? How did God re-establish the Sabbath-rest? What is the
symbol of the Sabbath-rest? How can we enter God's rest?

9. How can we practically make every effort to enter God's rest? (4:10-13)

10. When and to whom must we give account for our inner thoughts and
attitude of heart as well as for our actions? Why is disobeying God's
word such a serious matter?
