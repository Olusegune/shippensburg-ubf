IN THE FOOTSTEPS OF ABRAHAM'S FAITH

Romans 4:1-25
Key Verse: 4:3

1. Read verses 1-4. On what basis was Abraham forgiven his sins and made
right with God? What is the scriptural evidence for this? Why is it so
important that Abraham's righteousness was not earned by doing some good
work?

2. Read verses 5-8. According to David who is the really happy or blessed
man? (Ps 32:1-5) What was the common faith of David and Abraham? What
does this mean to you?

3. Read verses 9-12. Why is it so important that Abraham's faith was
credited to him as righteousness before he was circumcised? Of what is
circumcision a sign? What does it mean to walk in the footsteps of
Abraham's faith? Why is it important to know that we are saved only by
God's grace?

4. Read verses 13-17. What promise did Abraham and his offspring receive?
What do you think this means? Why must the promise be received by faith
rather than through the law? Why is this important to us?

5. Read verses 17-25. What did Abraham believe about God that his spiritual
descendants also believe? Why and how did Abraham become the father of
many nations?

6. What was Abraham's personal problem? How did he apply his faith to his
own problem? How did he give glory to God? What do you personally learn
from his example about justification through faith?
