THE TABERNACLE

Exodus 25-31; 35-40
Key Verse: 25:8

"Then have them make a sanctuary for me, and I will dwell among them."

STUDY QUESTIONS

Read 25:1-9. What kind of offerings did God want from his people? What was
the purpose of the sanctuary? Why must it be made exactly like God's
pattern?

Read 25:21-22. What is in the ark? What is on top of it? What does the ark
and its cover teach about God's justice and mercy? (Ro 3:23-25) About God's
word?

(25:23-40) What do the table and the lampstand teach us about God? (Compare
Jn 6:35; 1Jn 1:5; Jn 8:12) Describe the tabernacle and location of the
items in it (26; 30:1-8). How does it point to Jesus?

What was the purpose of the bronze altar? (27) How does this point to
Christ? (Lev 17:11; Heb 9:14,22; Jn 1:29)

Read 28:1-5. What was the point of clothing Aaron with such beautiful
garments? (Compare Ro 13:14) What can we learn about the work of a mediator
from the Ephod and the Breastpiece? (28) How does the priestly work point
to Jesus? (1Ti 2:5,6; Heb 9:11-12)

When the time came to build the tabernacle, what spiritual preparation did
Moses make? (35:1-3,5,21) How did the people respond? How were the chief
workers prepared? (35:30-31)

Read 40:1-8. Describe the tabernacle. Read 40:33-36. After the work was
finished according to God's pattern how did God show his acceptance? What
does this tabernacle teach us about the meaning of being a kingdom of
priests?
