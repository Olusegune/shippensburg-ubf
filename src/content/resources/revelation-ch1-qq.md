"LOOK, HE IS COMING"

Revelation 1:1-20
Key Verse: 1:7

Look, he is coming with the clouds, and every eye will see him, even
those who pierced him; and all the peoples of the earth will mourn
because of him.  So shall it be! Amen.

1. The word "revelation" (or apocalypse) means "to bring to light what was
formerly hidden or veiled." Who and what will be unveiled by the
revelation given to John? (1-5) How can we take the word of prophecy to
heart? (3)

2. What does it mean Christ freed us from our sins and has made us to be a
kingdom and priests to serve God? (5-6)

3. John presents a vision of Jesus who is coming (7) as well as who is
already and always here (8). What would it be like to have only one of
these two perspectives? In what ways has a knowledge of Christ's coming
encouraged you in the midst of suffering? Think about God who is the
Alpha and the Omega. (8)

4. What was John's situation at the time he received the revelation? How
does his situation make you want to hear what he has to say? (9-11)

5. The seven churches in Asia are compared to seven golden lampstands
(12,20). What does this tell us about the function of the churches? What
is Jesus' relationship to these churches? (13)

6. Think about the vision of Jesus. Which images impresses you most with
the magnificence of Jesus, and why? (13-16) How did Jesus encourage John
to overcome his fear in his situation? (17-20)
