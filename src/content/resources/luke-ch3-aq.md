PREPARE THE WAY FOR THE LORD

Luke 3:1-20
Key Verse: 3:4

1. Who does Luke mention as the political and religious leaders of John's
time? (1-2a) What does this tell us about those times? Who was John? (2b,
1:13-17)

2. Where was John when the word of God came to him? (2b) What does "the
word of God came to him" mean? When the word of God came to John, what
did he do? (3)

3. What is a "baptism of repentance for the forgiveness of sins?" (3) Why
did John preach this? (4; Isa 40:3-5) How can we prepare for the Lord?
(5) What is the true repentance? (4-5)

4. How is preparing the way related to God's salvation? (6) How did the
crowds respond to John's preaching? (7a) How did he rebuke them? (7b) How
did he challenge them? (8a)

5. Why are human credentials not so important? (8b) What happens to those
who do not produce good fruit? (9) How did the crowd respond? (10)

6. What does producing fruit in keeping with repentance mean to each
person? (11-14) How does this apply to us?

7. What were the people wondering about John? (15) How did he contrast his
ministry with that of Jesus? (16-17) What was the good news John
proclaimed? (18) Why did Herod lock John up in prison? (19-20)
