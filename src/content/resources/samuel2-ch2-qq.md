DAVID BECOMES KING OVER ALL ISRAEL

2 Samuel 2:8-5:5
Key Verse: 5:2b-3

1. After David became king of Judah, how did Ish-Bosheth become king of
Israel? (2:8-9) Why did Abner do this? (2:8a; 1Sa 14:50-51; 17:55) Note
the length of their reigns (2:10-11). Where did their first conflict take
place? (2:12-13 and see a map) What did Abner suggest to do, and what
happened? (2:14-17)

2. How did Joab's brother Asahel die? (2:18-23) How did Abner and Joab
bring a stop to the battle? (2:24-29) What were the results? (2:30-3:1)
How did the sons born to David in Hebron illustrate the strengthening of
his kingdom? (3:2-5) In light of this battle, how was God working?

3. What drove Abner to transfer the kingdom to David? (3:6-11) On what
basis did he do this? (3:9) At this time, what was the broader
significance of David's bringing Saul's daughter Michal back as his wife?
(3:12-16) How did Abner prepare for David to become king of Israel, and
how did David respond? (3:17-21)

4. How did Joab almost ruin the new peace, and what was his motive? (3:22-
27) How did David curse him and why? (3:28-29,38-39) How did David
conduct Abner's funeral, and what did the people realize? (3:31-37) Why
was this important?

5. After Abner's death, what happened to Ish-Bosheth? (4:1,5-7) How did
David react when Ish-Bosheth's head was brought to him? (4:8-12) Review
how David dealt with Abner, Joab and Rekab and Baanah. What can we learn
from this about his kingship?

6. What did all the tribes of Israel remember about David and about God's
promise? (5:1-2) What can we learn here about the kind of leader God
wants for his people? Why did God want a shepherd-like king for his
people? (1Sa 12:12b; 13:14; Ps 23:1; Isa 40:11; Eze 34:22-24)

7. When the elders of Israel came to him at Hebron, what did King David do
and why? (5:3) What did they do, and how was God fulfilling his promise
in this? Why did God take time in making David king of all Israel? Note
the description of David's reign (5:4-5).
