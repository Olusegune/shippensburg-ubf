WHAT IS A GOOD LIFE?

Ecclesiastes 6:1-12
Key Verse: 6:12

1. What evil (or tragedy) does the author observe under the sun? (1-2) Why
would God not allow some people to enjoy their worldly success? (2; 5:19;
3:14)

2. What is the life story of a man which the author describes in verses 3-
6? What is the point of this hypothetical story?

3. Read verses 7-9. If neither the fruit of our labor nor worldly wisdom
can truly satisfy our soul, then what can? Why do we need a right
relationship with God in order to satisfy our soul?

4. Given the limitations of humans, how futile is it for them to argue
against their Creator who knows their past, present and future? (10-12)
What should we do knowing that God is in control of everything?
