JESUS' ENTRY INTO JERUSALEM

Mark 11:1-19
Key Verse: 11:9b

"Hosanna!" Blessed is he who comes in the name of the Lord!

1. Read verses 1-2. Why was Jesus going to Jerusalem? (10:45) When they
were in the Jerusalem area what errand did Jesus give two of his
disciples? What was potentially difficult about this command?

2. Read verses 3-6. What did the disciples say and do? What was the
response of the people standing there? Why is "the Lord needs it"
sufficient reason? What does this event teach about the Lordship of
Christ?

3. Read verses 7-10. Why did he want to enter Jerusalem in this way? (Zech
9:9-10) Describe the people's response. What does "Hosanna" mean? Why is
this called the "Triumphal Entry"?

4. Read verse 11. After arriving in Jerusalem, where did Jesus go? Why?
What did he see? Then where did he go? Read verses 12-14. What did Jesus
do? How was the fig tree like the temple? (Compare Ro 3:12; Isa 5:2)

5. Read verses 15-19. What did Jesus do? Why was he so furious? Think about
God's purpose for his city and people (Isa 2:3; Ex 19:6a). What did Jesus
teach about the true meaning of the temple? (17) For what should God's
people pray?
