BE READY FOR THE LORD'S COMING

Luke 12:35-59
Key Verse: 12:40

1. Read verses 35-37. For what must we be ready? How can we be ready? Why
will it be good for those servants whose master finds them ready and
watching? (Lk 22:28-30)

2. Read verses 38-40. With what attitude should we wait for the Son of Man
to come? To what future event does this refer? (1Th 4:16-18; Mt 25:31)
What does it mean to all people that Jesus comes again? What is the hope
without which we cannot be ready? (1Pe 1:3,4; Ro 8:17,18) How might our
attitude be different if we knew the exact time of his coming?

3. Read verses 41-46. What did Peter ask? Why? With what question did Jesus
counter? What characterizes the faithful manager (servant)? The
unfaithful one? What happens to each? What does it mean to be ready?

4. Read verses 47-48. What is the contrast between the two servants here?
What is Jesus' point?

5. Read verses 49-53. What does Jesus mean by "bring fire on the earth" and
"baptism"? (Lk 3:16,17) What does it mean that he brings division, not
peace?

6. Read verses 54-59. To whom does Jesus speak? Why does he call them
hypocrites? What does it mean to "judge for yourselves what is right"?
How are these teachings connected?
