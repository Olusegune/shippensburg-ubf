GOD CALLS MOSES

Exodus 2:1-4:31
Key Verse: 4:12

"Now go; I will help you speak and will teach you what to say."

STUDY QUESTIONS

Read 2:1-4. Why did Moses' mother have to hide him? (1:22) What did she do?
Read verses 5-10. How did God bless her faith and save her baby? How did he
provide for Moses' spiritual and human education? (Compare Ac 7:21,22) What
did his name mean?

Read 2:11-15. What event changed the course of Moses' life? What does this
reveal about his need for God's training? Read 2:16-22; 3:1. Describe his
life in Midian. What did he learn from taking care of sheep for 40 years?
(See Nu 12:3)

Why did God look on the Israelites with concern? (2:23-25) Read 3:1-5.
Where and how did God reveal himself to Moses? Why did Moses' vision need
rekindling? What is holy ground?

Read 3:6-12. How did God introduce himself? (6) What does this mean? Why
was God concerned? What had God come to do? How did he plant hope in slave
people? What was Moses' mission? His response? God's promise and
instructions?

Read 3:13-22. How was Moses to introduce God to the people? (13-15) How was
he to remind them of God's covenant? (16-17; Ge 15:13-21) How would God
deal with the Egyptians?

Read 4:1-9. How would God use the staff in Moses' hand? Read verses 10-17.
How did God answer Moses' plea of lack of eloquence?

Read 4:18-31. What happened on the way back to Egypt? (18-26) What did this
event teach Moses? How did the Israelites respond to Moses and his message?
(27-31) What does this teach us?
