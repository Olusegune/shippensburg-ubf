INTRODUCTION TO ECCLESIATES

Ecclesiastes 12:13

Now all has been heard; here is the conclusion of the matter:
Fear God and keep his commandments, for this is the duty of all
mankind.

1. Who is the author of this book? (1:1) Who was his mother? (2Sa 11:1-
12:25) How could her story have affected his childhood and his
relationships with his half-brothers? (1Chr 3:1-9)

2. How did Solomon become king of Israel? (1Ki 1:1-53) What was King
David's charge to him? (1Ki 2:1-4) What did Solomon ask for from God (1Ki
3:4-9)? How did God bless him? (1Ki 3:10-13) How successful was Solomon?
(1Ki 4:20-34, 6:11-14, 8:1-2, 65-66, 10:6-13)

3. What caused Solomon's downfall? (1Ki 11:1-13; 9:3-9) How did God punish
Solomon and his people? (1Ki 11:14-40) Though the Bible is not explicit
about his final years, what can you surmise about his repentance before
God from his writings in Proverbs and Ecclesiastes? (Pr 1:1-7; Ecc 12:13-
14)

4. In what sense was Solomon well-equipped to write a book of wisdom? (Ecc
12:9-10) Read Ecclesiastes 12:13. Why is it important to study this book
with this conclusion in mind?
