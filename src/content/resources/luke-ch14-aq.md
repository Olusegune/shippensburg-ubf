COME TO THE GREAT BANQUET

Luke 14:1-24
Key Verse: 14:23

1. Read verses 1-6. Why did a prominent Pharisee invite Jesus to his
banquet? What was his plot? Why were the religious leaders carefully
watching Jesus? What was the question he asked them? How did Jesus show
God's mercy and expose their hypocrisy? (4-5; Lk 13:15-17)

2. Read verses 7-11. What did Jesus notice in the banquet? What did he
teach the guests about being humble?  (11, Jas 4:6,10) What does it mean
to you personally to be humble? (Php 2:5-10; Mk 10:45)

3. Read verses 12-14. What advice did Jesus give his host? Whom should we
invite to parties? Why? (Mt 25:35-40)

4. Read verses 15-24. What is this parable about? Who were the invited
guests? (Ro 9:1-5) What excuses did invited guests give? Why? Can you
think of modern parallels? Who finally sat down at the banquet table?

5. Read verses 23-24 again. Why does God invite us to the banquet of the
kingdom of God? Why does he want us to invite other people to the feast?
What is the kingdom of God like?
