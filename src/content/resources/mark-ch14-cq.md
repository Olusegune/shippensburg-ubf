JESUS PRAYS AT GETHSEMANE

Mark 14:27-52
Key Verse: 14:36

"'Abba, Father,' he said, 'everything is possible for you. Take this
cup from me. Yet not what I will, but what you will.'"

1. Read verses 27-28. According to Jesus, how would this scripture be
fulfilled? What promise did Jesus give his disciples? How could this
promise help them?

2. Read verses 29-31. How did Peter respond? The others? Did Peter know
himself? What hope did Jesus have for him, in spite of his weakness? (Lk
22:31-32)

3. Read verses 32-34. Who did Jesus take with him to Gethsemane? What did
he ask them to do? Why could they not keep watch with him? (37-38, 41)
Why was Jesus so distressed? So lonely? How is Jesus' humanity revealed
here?

4. Read verses 35-42. How did Jesus address God? What does this show about
his relationship with God? What was his prayer topic? What did he mean by
"this hour"? "This cup"? What reveals his inner conflict? His humanness?
His spiritual victory?

5. Read verses 43-46. Who was Judas? How did he participate in Jesus'
arrest? What was the result of his following Jesus without any commitment
to him?

6. Read verses 47-52. How did those standing near react when Jesus was
seized? How did Jesus respond? What did the disciples do that fulfilled
scripture? Who might the young man be? (51-52) Why the great contrast
between Jesus and his disciples?
