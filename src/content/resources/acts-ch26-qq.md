I STAND HERE TO TESTIFY ABOUT JESUS

Acts 26:1-32
Key Verses: 26:22-23

1. Who was King Agrippa? What did Paul know about him? (1-3; 26-27)

2. What did the Jews know about Paul's background? (4-5) How was Paul's
hope in Jesus related to his Jewish background? (6-7) How was his present
situation related to that hope? What was Paul's hope? (8)

3. When Paul lived as a strict and dedicated Jew, what had been his view of
Jesus and his followers? (9) To what lengths had he gone to eradicate
Christianity? (10-11)

4. What had happened to change Paul? (12-15) What did Jesus say to him?
What does it mean that he was kicking against the goads? (14) What did
Jesus command Paul to do? (16) What did he promise? (17-18) How did God
want to bless the world?

5. How did Paul respond to Jesus' command? (19-21) Read verses 22-23. What
was Paul's testimony about Jesus? Why was it important that it was rooted
in the Scriptures?

6. Why did Festus interrupt? (24) What was Paul's reply? (25-27) What was
his prayer for Agrippa? (28-29) What did the king and the governor
conclude about Paul? (30-32)
