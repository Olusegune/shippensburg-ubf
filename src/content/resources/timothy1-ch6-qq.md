FIGHT THE GOOD FIGHT OF THE FAITH

1 Timothy 6:1-21
Key Verse: 6:12

1. Read verses 1-2. What should be a slave's attitude toward his master and
toward his job? Why? What did it mean for a slave to regard his master as
a brother? How can these principles be applied today?

2. What's the problem of a person who deliberately teaches false doctrines
because he doesn't agree to sound doctrine and godly teaching? (3-5) Why
do some people think that godliness is a means to financial gain?

3. Read verses 6-10. According to Paul, what is great gain? Why? What kind
of pitfalls do those who want to get rich fall into? What is a root of
all kinds of evil? Why?

4. From what must the man of God flee? What must he pursue? Think about
Jesus. How does his character and lifestyle exemplify these things?

5. What does it mean to fight the good fight of the faith? How can we do
so? What is the good confession? How did Jesus set the example? (12-13)

6. Until when must one fight the good fight? (14) What does Paul say about
God? How does he praise him?

7. What command must Timothy give to those who are rich? Why is it wise not
to put one's hope in worldly wealth? (17) What is the next command? What
are true riches and wealth? What is the life that is truly life? How can
we take hold of it? (18-19)

8. How can Timothy guard what has been entrusted to his care?
