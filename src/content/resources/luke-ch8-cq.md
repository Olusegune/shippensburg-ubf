WHERE IS YOUR FAITH?

Luke 8:22-25
Key Verse: 8:25

1.    Read verse 22. Why might Jesus have suggested that they go across the
lake? (cf. 23a; Mk 6:31) What does this suggest about Jesus' life and
ministry?

2.    Read verse 23. What did Jesus do as they were sailing across the
lake? What unexpected thing happened? Describe their situation. How could
Jesus continue sleeping?

3.    Read verse 24a. What did the disciples do and say? What does this
reveal about them? What does it mean that they called him "Master,
Master"? Read verse 24b. How did Jesus handle the storm? What does this
reveal about him?

4.    Read verse 25a. What did Jesus say to his disciples? How did he see
the problem? In what sense is fear the opposite of faith? Why is it
essential that Christians overcome fear?

5.    Read verse 25b. What was the response of the disciples? What kind of
fear is this? What did they realize about Jesus? What kind of faith does
he want his disciples to have? How does God use the storms of life to
train us?
