THE GOSPEL OF THE RESURRECTION

1 Corinthians 15:1-11
Key Verses: 15:3-4

"For what I received I passed on to you as of first importance: that
Christ died for our sins according to the Scriptures, that he was
buried, that he was raised on the third day according to the
Scriptures..."

1. Read verses 1-2. What relationship does Paul have to those to whom he
writes? Why was it necessary for them to be reminded of the gospel again?
(What were the problems in the Corinthian church?)

2. According to verses 3-4, what is the gospel? (Lk 2:10b) Why is it such
good news that Christ died for our sins? Why does Paul say "according to
the Scriptures"? Why does he consider the gospel to be of first
importance?

3. What does it mean that "Christ died for our sins"? What is sin? How
serious are the consequences of sin? How extensive a problem is sin? (cf.
Ro 3:23,10,11; Ro 6:23; Heb 9:27; 2 Co 5:10)

4. How did God provide a solution to man's sin problem? (Jn 1:29, 3:16,
5:24, 1Pe 2:24) What must we do? Why can only Jesus save us? (Ac 4:12; Jn
14:6)

5. What does Jesus' resurrection teach us about God? About Jesus? (1Co
15:20-28, 45-58)

6. What is the hope given us by the resurrection of Jesus? (1Co 15:17-19,
42-44, 45-58; 1Pe 1:3,4)

7. When and where did the risen Jesus appear to Peter? To the Twelve? To
the others? Why was it so important to have witnesses to the
resurrection? How did they witness to Jesus' resurrection?

8. When and how did Paul meet the risen Jesus? How did the risen Jesus
change him? How was his life a testimony to the fact that Jesus rose from
the dead? What was his life purpose? Of what was he confident?
