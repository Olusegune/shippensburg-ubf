WARNINGS AGAINST POLITICAL AND SPIRITUAL LEADERS

Micah 2:1-3:12
Key Verse: 3:8

But as for me, I am filled with power, with the Spirit of the LORD,
and with justice and might, to declare to Jacob his transgression, to
Israel his sin.

1. Look at 2:1-5. How do men plot evil on their beds? What were some
specific social evils that Micah observed? (2; cf. Isa 1:16-17,23) What
was God planning against the people who carried out evil because it was
in their power to do it? (3-5)

2. Look at 2:6-11. How did false prophets advise Micah to keep quiet and
not talk about unpleasant things? Why were the false prophets popular?
(11)

3. Read 2:12-13. What was God's promise for the remnant of Israel in the
day of catastrophe? Who were the remnant of Israel?

4. Look at 3:1-4. How did the leaders of Israel use their power to exploit
the people for their own selfish gain? How were they like wolves rather
than shepherds for God's sheep? Why would God not answer them when they
would cry out to the Lord? Why should we love good and hate evil?

5. Look at 3:5-7. How does Micah describe the prophets who worked for
money? (5) How would the sun set for them and night come over them? (6)
How devastating would it be if there is no answer from God (no word of
God), no vision or hope?

6. Read 3:8-12. How was Micah filled with power and with the Spirit of the
Lord because he spoke God's word with courage and truth? (8) How did the
leaders of Israel despise justice and distort all that is right? How did
materialism permeate the hearts of the leaders? How did they deceive
themselves? What was God's warning against them?
