LOVE YOUR NEIGHBOR

Deuteronomy 24:5-26:19
Key Verse: 24:19

1. What consideration should be given to a recently married man? Why?
(24:5) Why shouldn't millstones be taken as collateral for a debt? (6)
How serious is human trafficking? (7)

2. How was leprosy to be treated? (8-9) Why? How should lender show respect
to borrower? (10-13) How should employer treat his employees? (14-15)
Why?

3. Who is ultimately responsible for his sin? (16) How should God's people
treat the foreigner, the fatherless or the widow? (17-18) How should they
help the underprivileged neighbors? Why? (19-22)

4. What were the instructions on settling personal disputes? (25:1-3) Why
they should not muzzle an ox while it was treading out the grain? (4) Why
should a man who would not take responsibility for having children for a
dead brother should be shamed and dishonored? (5-10)

5. What was the spirit of the law given in verses 11-12? Why should they
use honest weights and measures? (13-16) Why should the Israelites deal
strictly with the Amalekites? (17-19; Ex 17:8-16)

6. What were the Isralites to do with the firstfruits in the Promised Land?
What should they say to the priest in office? (26:1-4) What should they
confess to the Lord (5-11) What was the purpose of their third-year tithe
and what should be their prayers to the Lord? (12-15)

7. How should God's people observe the Lord's commands? (16) What does it
mean that they would declare that the Lord was their God, and God would
declare that they were his people, his treasured possession? (17-19) How
can we become God's treasured possession, a people holy to the Lord our
God?
