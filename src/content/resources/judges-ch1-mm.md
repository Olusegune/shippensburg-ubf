of the Judges? Look at verses 2:10-15. Then the Israelites did evil in the eyes of the Lord

plundered them. He sold them to their enemies all around, whom they were no longer
able to resist. Whenever Israel went out to fight, the hand of the Lord was against
them to defeat them, just as he had sworn to them. They were in great distress.
