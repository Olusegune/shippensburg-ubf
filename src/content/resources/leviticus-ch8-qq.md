THE HOLY PRIESTLY DUTIES

Leviticus 8:1-10:20
Key Verses: 10:10-11

* The ordination of the priests (Chapter 8)

1. Read verses 1-9. What did the Lord tell Moses to do before anointing
Aaron and his sons? What were the elements required for the priesthood?
Think about the meaning of each.

2. Read verses 10-29. What and whom did Moses anoint? (10-13) What is the
spiritual meaning of this anointing? How did Moses sacrifice a sin
offering (14-17), a burnt offering (18-21) and an ordination offering
(22-29) for Aaron and his sons? Why do the priests need such sacrifices?
(Heb 5:1-3; 7:26-28)

3. Read verses 30-36. Why did Moses sprinkle on Aaron and his sons the
anointing oil mixed with blood? (30) Why did God command them to eat the
meat and the bread of the ordination offerings? (31-32) Why did God
command them to stay at the entrance to the tent of meeting for seven
days? (33-36; Ex 29:35-37)

* The priests begin their ministry (Chapter 9)

4. When did Moses summon Aaron, his sons and the elders of Israel? (1) What
did he tell Aaron to do for himself and the Israelites? (2-6) What was
the purpose? (4,6)

5. Describe the details of how Aaron made atonement for himself. (7-14) And
for the people. (15-21) Why was it important for him to offer the
sacrifices "in the prescribed way"? (16) What did he do after all was
done? (22)

6. What did Moses and Aaron do together? (23) How did God accept the
offerings? How did the people respond? (24) In what sense was the worship
experience real to them?

7. How many times does the word "commanded" appear in this chapter? What
does this teach us about how to worship the Lord? How can we worship God
"in the Spirit and in truth"? (Jn 4:24)

* Importance of the holy priesthood (Chapter 10)

8. Read verses 1-3. Why and how did Aarons' sons Nadab and Abihu die? What
was their offense before the Lord? (1-2; 9) Why did God punish them so
severely? (3)

9. Read verses 4-11. How were the dead bodies carried out? (4-5)? What
shouldn't the holy priests do even though their family members died? (6-
8) Why (7, Lk 9:59-60)? What must the holy priests not do? (8-9) What is
an important priestly duty? (10-11)

10. Read verses 12-20. What were the priests' shares of the offerings and
where were they to eat them? (12-15) Why was Moses angry with Aaron's
remaining sons? (16-18) What was Aaron's reply? (19-20) What do you
learn about the holy priestly duties in this passage?
