"BEHOLD, I AM COMING SOON!"

Revelation 22:1-21
Key Verse: 22:7

"Behold, I am coming soon! Blessed is he who keeps the words of the
prophecy in this book."

1. What did John see flowing from the throne of God and of the Lamb? (1)
What was on each side of the river? How many fruits did the tree bear and
when? What are the leaves of the tree for? (2; Eze 47:12)

2. What will no longer exist in the city? (cf. Ge 3:17-19) What will be
there instead? (3) What is said about the servants of God and of the
Lamb? (3-5) What about night and the light of the sun? (5)

3. What did the angel say to John about the visions which he has seen? (6;
cf. 19:9)

4. Look at verse 7. What promise and assurance is given to those who keep
the words of God? What did John try to do when he heard and saw these
things? (8) What did the angel tell John? (9)

5. What is John told regarding the prophecy of this book? Why? (10) What do
you think verse 11 means?

6. Look at verse 12. What promise and assurance is given? How does Jesus
describe himself? (13) What blessedness is promised to those who wash
their robes? (14) Who will be outside the city? (15)

7. Why had Jesus sent his angel? (16) How does Jesus describe himself? (16;
cf. Isa 11:1,10) Who extends the invitation to the world to come to
Jesus? Who else is to extend this invitation? (17) What is offered?

8. What warning is given to those who hear the words of the prophecy of
this book? (18-19) What promise is given by him who testifies of these
things? (20) What two prayers does John express as he closes the book?
(20-21) What do you need to change to be prepared for Jesus' coming?
