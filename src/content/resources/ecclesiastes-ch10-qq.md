WORDS AND DEEDS OF THE WISE AND THE FOOL

Ecclesiastes 10:1-20
Key Verse: 10:2

1. How are the ways of the wise different from those of the fool? (2-3; Ja
1:5, Ps 14:1) Think about how a little folly can destroy a person's
honorable life. (1) How do the wise respond to the foolish behavior of
those in high positions? (4-7; Eph 6:5-8)

2. While our work does not define us, it is important part of our life. How
can a wise person plan carefully in doing his job in this world? (8-11)
Share your examples of a wise planning that resulted in success in your
work.

3. How do the wise and fools use words differently? (12-14) How does the
toil of fools weary them? (15) Who is a foolish person in the Bible?

4. Think about how the whole country suffers when a leader is immature and
lacks a sense of responsibility for the people. (16-17) How does a
person's laziness affect his life and his family's? (18; Pr 20:4)

5. What is the fool's outlook of life? (19) Why is it important to mind our
thoughts and words even in private moments? (20)
