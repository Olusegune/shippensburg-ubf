SUCH GREAT FAITH

Luke 7:1-10
Key Verse: 7:9

1. Read verses 1-2. What was a centurion? What was the situation of
servants or slaves in the Roman Empire? What is unusual about this
centurion's attitude toward his dying servant? How does he reveal the
image of Jesus? (Jn 10:11)

2. Read verse 3. What might the centurion have heard about Jesus? What hope
did he find for his dying servant? How did he bring this problem to
Jesus?

3. Read verses 4-6a. What was the attitude of the Jewish elders toward
Jesus? Toward the centurion? Describe the relationship between the elders
and the centurion. How was such a relationship possible?

4. Read verses 6b-8. What did the centurion realize as Jesus was on the way
to his house? Describe his attitude toward Jesus and Jesus' word. What
was his understanding of the principle of authority? Think about how he
came to have this kind of faith in Jesus? (Ro 10:17; Eph 2:8-9)

5. Read verses 9-10. How did Jesus react to the centurion's words? What did
Jesus see in the centurion? (cf. Mt 15:28) What happened to the dying
servant? Think about what Jesus would say about your faith.
