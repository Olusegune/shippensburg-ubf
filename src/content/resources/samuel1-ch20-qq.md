A TRUE FRIENDSHIP IN THE LORD

1 Samuel 20:1-42
Key Verse: 20:42

1. After seeking Samuel's spiritual help, how did David explain his
situation to Jonathan? (1-3) What pain did David bear to keep his
friendship with Jonathan?

2. What kind of help did David ask Jonathan? (5-8) How willing was Jonathan
to help David? (4,9)

3. What did Jonathan agree to do and how would this help David? (10-13a) On
what basis did Jonathan make a covenant with David? What was the content
of the covenant? (13b-17)

4. How did Jonathan make a secret sign to communicate with David? (18-23)

5. What did Jonathan discover about Saul's intention? (24-33) How did
Jonathan respond? (34) How did Jonathan warn David (35-40)

6. In their grief, what did Jonathan and David do to confirm their
friendship? What can we learn here about the friendship in the Lord? How
is this like Jesus' friendship with us? (Jn 15:13-15) How is the
friendship in the Lord different from other friendships?
