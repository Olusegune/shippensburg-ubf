KINSMAN-REDEEMER

Ruth 4:1-21
Key Verse: 4:14

The women said to Naomi: "Praise be to the LORD, who this day has not
left you without a kinsman-redeemer. May he become famous throughout
Israel!"

* REDEMPTION IS COSTLY (1-12)

1. Read verses 1-4. Why did Boaz convene a meeting of the town elders and
the kinsman-redeemer? What did he say to the kinsman-redeemer? Why do you
think Naomi wanted to sell the piece of property? What was the relative's
reply?

2. Read verses 5-8. What additional obligation did Boaz mention to the
kinsman-redeemer? (Dt 25:5-10) What did he say in response? Why did he
refuse to redeem the property? How might the act of redemption endanger
his own estate?

3. Read verses 9-12. What did Boaz announce to the elders and all the
people? How does his redemptive act remind us of God's grace? In what
sense was Boaz foreshadow of Jesus Christ? (Isa 59:20; Ro 3:23-24) How
did the elders bless Boaz's decision? Why did they mention Rachel, Leah
and Tamar?

* GOD BLESSES RUTH'S FAITHFULNESS (13-17)

4. Read verses 13-15. How did God bless the marriage between Boaz and Ruth?
What can we learn from the women's prayer for Naomi? (14-15)

5. Read verses 16-17. What was so significant about Ruth's son becoming the
grandfather of David? Why was this a sign of God's redemptive purpose for
all people of the world that he chose a Gentile woman to be the direct
ancestress of Jesus? (Mt 1:5-6)
