LOVE AND OBEY THE LORD FOR YOUR OWN GOOD

Deuteronomy 10:12-11:32
Key Verses: 10:12-13

1. What was the purpose of God when he asked Israel to love and obey him?
(12-13) Why did he choose them above all nations? (14-15) What must his
people do to show their love for the Lord? (16-22)

2. What were the great things that the Lord did that the Israelites saw
with their own eyes? How were they related to the command "Love and obey
the Lord"? (11:1-7) What blessings would they receive by observing all
the commands of the Lord? (8-9)

3. In what way would the Promised Land be different from the land of Egypt?
(10-12) What abundant life would they enjoy if they would love and serve
the Lord with all their heart? (13-15) On the other hand, what would
happen to them if they would worship other gods? (16-17)

4. To what extent should God's people love the word of God? (18-20; Dt 6:6-
9). What blessings would they receive when they love the Lord and obey
his commands? (21-25)

5. What were the two choices set before God's people? (26) What would
determine whether they would receive the blessing or the curse in the
Promised Land? (27-30) What should they do? (31-32)
