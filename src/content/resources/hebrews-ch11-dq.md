FAITH THAT OVERCOMES THE WORLD

Hebrews 11:29-40
Key Verse: 11:29

"By faith the people passed through the Red Sea as on dry land; but
when the Egyptians tried to do so, they were drowned."

* THE ACTS OF FAITH (29-31)

1.    Right after the Exodus, what obstacle did the Israelites face and how
did they overcome it? (29)  Why were the Egyptians drowned? (29b)  Think
about the difference between faith and test.

2.    In what respect was Jericho important to the Israelites as they began
to conquer Canaan?  What were the difficulties they faced? (Jos 6)  Why
was it impractical and foolish, from a human point of view, to march
around the fortress?  How did Jericho fall?

3.    Who was Rahab?  How could she be saved?  What can you learn from God
who saved a Gentile prostitute?

* THE HEROES OF FAITH (32-40)

4.    Who are the other heroes of faith that the author doesn't have time
to tell about? (32)  What are the acts of faith that these heroes of
faith did? (33-35a)

5.    What other heroes of faith does the author tells about? (35b-38)
What kind of suffering did they endure?  In what respect are they
victors?

6.    What did the heroes of faith receive?  What are they yet to receive?
(39)  How would they be made perfect? (40)
