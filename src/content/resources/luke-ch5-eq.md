NEW WINE INTO NEW WINESKINS

Luke 5:33-39
Key Verse: 5:38

1. Read verse 33. Who criticized Jesus' disciples? Why did they do so? What
do you know about the religious activities of John's disciples? The
disciples of the Pharisees? (Mt 6:5,16) How might this criticism have
affected Jesus' disciples?

2. Read verse 34. How did Jesus defend his disciples? What did Jesus teach
about himself and his relationship with his people? (Rev 19:6-7; 21:2-3)
What do you learn about the characteristic of life in Jesus? Why might
the feast at Levi's house be like a heavenly celebration? (Lk 15:1-7)

3. Read verse 36. Why can't a new patch be put on an old garment? Read
verse 37. Why can't new wine be put into old wineskins? In the context of
this passage, what is the new wine? (Heb 4:12) New wineskins? Old wine?
Old wineskins?

4. How were Jesus' first disciples like new wineskins? (5:5, 5:28) What are
the characteristics of new-wineskin-like people? How were the Pharisees
like old wineskins? (39) What are the characteristics of old-wineskin-
like people?

5. Read verse 38. What kind of people must we be to contain the new wine?
How can you be like a new wineskin?
