RUTH MEETS BOAZ

Ruth 2:1-23
Key Verse: 2:12

"May the LORD repay you for what you have done. May you be richly
rewarded by the LORD, the God of Israel, under whose wings you have come
to take refuge."

1. Read verses 1-4. What did Ruth initiate to do to care for her destitute
mother-in-law? Why was this dangerous, especially for a Moabite woman in
Judah? (22) To whose harvest field did Ruth happen to go? Who was Boaz
and what kind of person was he? (1,4)

2. Read verses 5-9. Describe the meeting between Ruth and Boaz. How did
Boaz show kindness to Ruth despite her human background? How did he
provide her water and protection? (9)

3. Read verses 10-16. What did Boaz say when Ruth exclaimed, "Why have I
found such favor in your eyes that you notice me-a foreigner?" How was
Boaz moved by her loyalty and her faith in God? What does it mean to take
refuge under the wings of God? How was Ruth different from most people
who expect kindness and become resentful when they don't get it? (10,13)
How did Boaz continue to show his kindness and generosity?

4. Read verses 17-18. What can we learn from Ruth who worked hard all day
long? (7,17)

5. Read verses 19-20. When Naomi found out it was Boaz who had shown
kindness to Ruth, how did she react? (20) What is a kinsman-redeemer? (Dt
25:5-10; Lev 25:25,47-49; Jer 32:6-25)

6. Read verses 21-23. What did Naomi tell Ruth and what did Ruth do? How
was God working behind the scene to accomplish his redemptive purpose?
(Pr 16:9) Why can we have hope in God even in a hopeless situation?
