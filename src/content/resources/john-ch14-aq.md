JESUS IS THE WAY

John 14:1-14
Key Verse: 14:6

1. Read verse 1. Why were the disciples' hearts troubled and anxious?
(13:21, 33, 36-38) How can "believing" in God and in Jesus comfort their
troubled hearts?  What does it mean to "believe in God" and to believe in
Jesus?

2. Read verses 2-3. Why does Jesus speak of the "Father's house" instead of
the kingdom of heaven? What did he mean that he was going to prepare a
place for them?

3. How does the promise of the "Father's house" give us true hope and
direction? Why is it important to have hope in the kingdom of God in our
hearts?

4. Read verses 4-6. How did Thomas respond when Jesus said, "You know the
way"? Why? What did Jesus teach about the way to the Father's house? Read
verses 5-6. What does it mean that Jesus is the way?

5. What are some qualities that characterize truth? Why is Jesus "the
truth"? (Jn 8:31-32) What does this add to Jesus the way? Why is Jesus
"the life"? (Jn 1:4-5) What does this mean to us? (Jn 5:24; Mk 8:36,37)

6. Why is Jesus the only way to God? Read verses 7-8. Why did Philip ask
Jesus to show him the Father? What was Jesus' answer? Read verses 9-14.
How can we see and know the Father? How can we do his work? (12-14)
