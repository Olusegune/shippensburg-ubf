GOD'S GENEROSITY AND SOVEREIGNTY

Matthew 20:1-16
Key Verse: 20:1

"For the kingdom of heaven is like a landowner who went out early in
the morning to hire men to work in his vineyard."

1. Read verses 1-2. To what does Jesus compare the kingdom of God in this
parable? Why did the landowner hire workers? What did he agree to pay
them?

2. Read verses 3-7. When and why did he hire more workers? What does this
tell us about God and his kingdom? Why did the landowner hire workers
again and again? (Ge 1:28; 1Co 10:31; 2Th 3:10)

3. Read verses 8-9. How did the vineyard owner instruct the foreman to pay
the workers? What does this reveal about God?

4. Read verses 10-12. Why did the laborers whom he hired first grumble?
What did this show about them?

5. How did the landowner explain? Read verses 13-16. What does this show
about God's generosity? About his sovereignty? Why must we accept God's
sovereignty without complaints? What is Jesus teaching the disciples and
us about the kingdom of God in this parable?
