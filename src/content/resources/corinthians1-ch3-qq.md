WE ARE COWORKERS IN GOD'S SERVICE

1 Corinthians 3:1-23
Key Verse: 3:9

1. Why did Paul give Christians in Corinth only milk, not solid food? (1,2)
In what respect were they worldly (mere infants in Christ)? (3,4) What
are "milk" and "solid food"? What might be the reason that there was
jealousy and quarrelling among them?

2. How did God use Paul and Apollos in the Corinthian ministry? What was
the role of each servant? (5,6) Who makes the seed grow? In what sense
servants are nothing compared to God? (7) Think about how God is working
through his servants. (Php 2:12-13)

3. What does verse 8 teach us about different tasks that God's servants do?
Why should we respect one another in serving the Lord rather than become
jealous? What can we learn about the importance of coworking in God's
ministry? (9) How can we apply this to ourselves and our ministry?

4. In what sense was Paul an expert builder? (10,11) Why is the foundation
of a building so important? What is the only foundation for Christians or
the church of God? (1Co 15:3-4; Eph 2:20-22) What kind of material the
builders can use as they build? (12) How will the work of the builders be
tested? (13-15) What can we learn about the building God's church or
building one's life?

5. What does it mean we are God's temple? (16) What will happen to those
who cause divisions in the church? (17)

6. What false confidence did they have in worldly wisdom? (18) How does God
view such wisdom? (19-20) What was their motive in boasting? Why should
we not boast about human leaders? (21b-23) In light of this passage, what
is the ultimate solution to divisions in the church?
