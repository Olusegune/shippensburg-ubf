I APPEAL TO CAESAR!

Acts 25:1-27
Key Verse: 25:11

1. Who succeeded Felix as governor? (24:27) Why did Felix leave Paul in
prison? What did Festus do after arriving in the province? (1-2) What did
the Jews in Jerusalem request? Why? (3)

2. How did Festus respond to the request? (4-5) Describe the trial before
Festus--the accusations of the Jews and Paul's defense. (6-8)

3. Read verses 9-12. What option did Festus give Paul? What was Paul's
appeal and why did he make this request? Why was it so important that
Paul go to Rome?

4. How did Festus explain his dilemma to King Agrippa? (13-21) What
continued to be the focal point of the charges against Paul? What was
Agrippa's response? (22)

5. Read verses 23-27. What was Festus' problem in sending Paul to Rome?
What help did he request of King Agrippa? How can we see God working to
fulfill his promises and carry out his plan? (Ac 9:15; 19:21; 23:11)
