JESUS HEALS A MAN BORN BLIND

John 9:1-12
Key Verses: 9:3-4

1. What did Jesus' disciples ask him about the blind beggar? What were
their assumptions about God, about sin, and about suffering? In what
respect were they fatalistic? How did they identify with him?

2. Read verses 3-5. What does Jesus teach here about life's purpose? How
should we view life's sorrows and problems? Practically speaking, how can
one live for the glory of God? Can you think of some examples?

3. What does Jesus teach about the time to work for God? What did Jesus
teach about himself? (4-5) What difference does it make to accept Jesus?
(1:9)

4. Read verses 6-7. What did Jesus teach by using the mud and saliva? How
is his shepherd's heart revealed? Why do we need to learn Jesus' "spit
and mud" faith?

5. Why might the man have reacted negatively? What shows his faith? What
can we learn here about how to overcome fatalism and make things work
out? What was the man's testimony? (8-12)
