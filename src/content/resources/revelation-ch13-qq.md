THE TWO BEASTS

Revelation 13:1-18
Key Verse: 13:10b

This calls for patient endurance and faithfulness on the part of the
saints.

1. Describe the physical characteristics of the beast out of the sea. (1-3)
What clues does the Prophet Daniel give us to identify this beast? (Da
7:1-6,17)

2. What did the dragon give the beast from the sea? (2) How did the world
respond to the beast from the sea? (3-4)

3. What was given to the beast and how did the beast blaspheme? (5-6) What
else was granted to the beast and who would worship him? (7-8)

4. What warnings and encouragements are given to us? (9-10) What are
Christians' weapons in war against the devil?

5. Describe the beast coming out of the earth. (11) What does the beast
represent?

6. What does the beast do? Carefully think about his actions and what we
can learn from them. (12-14)

7. What other power was the beast given? (15) What did the beast require
everyone to receive? Why? (16-17)

8. What does it mean that the number of the beast, 666, is "man's number"?
Why does this call for wisdom? How can we recognize the mark of Satan and
overcome his deceptions?
